## Who We Are

TulsaGays.com is Tulsa's only dedicated LGBTQ+ community calendar and directory.
Every week we compile every queer-relevant event in the metro — gay bars, drag shows,
community meetings, arts events, Pride programming — and deliver it as a free weekly
email newsletter and a live, searchable website.

We are anonymous, independently operated, and have no outside funding. That means
we answer only to the community.

## Why advertise here?

**You can't buy this audience anywhere else.**
There is no other dedicated LGBTQ+ event guide in Tulsa. Our subscribers opted in
specifically to know what's happening in the queer community. They are not
a general audience who happen to include LGBTQ+ people — they are LGBTQ+ Tulsans
who are actively engaged with the community calendar.

**We do not accept anti-LGBTQ+ advertisers.** Full stop. That means your brand
is in genuinely safe company.

**We write your listing.** You don't need to produce copy. We write your description
in the Tulsa Gays voice (sassy, specific, community-grounded) and you approve it.
