```

[Top event name]
+ 4 more reasons to leave the house.

Full list in the newsletter.
Link in bio → sign up.
```

**Story 2 (after a good event has happened — post Monday or Tuesday):**

```
Get the list every Monday.
Link in bio.
```

**Story 3 (Pride season variant):**

```
Subscribe before Monday.
Link in bio →
```

## B. Reddit Posts — r/Tulsa, r/TulsaOklahoma
Post once per month. Lead with community value, not promotion.
Rotate these variants. Never post the same one twice in a row.

**Post 1: Event round-up (Monday or Tuesday)**
Title: `LGBTQ+ events in Tulsa this week — full list`
Body:
```
Compiled the full rundown for this week. Everything from drag shows to community
meetings to arts events across the metro.

Full list with times/venues/descriptions: [tulsagays.com link]

There's also a free weekly email newsletter if you want this in your inbox
every Monday before you need it.
```

**Post 2: Specific event highlight**
Title: `Anyone going to [event name] on [day]?`
Body:
```
Saw [event name] is happening [day] at [venue]. [1 sentence about why it's notable.]

Trying to gauge if there's a crowd going. Also been tracking the full LGBTQ+ event
calendar at tulsagays.com if anyone wants the whole week's list.
```

**Post 3: Community ask (builds goodwill, drives signups organically)**
Title: `What LGBTQ+ events/spaces in Tulsa should more people know about?`
Body:
```
Putting together a community-sourced list of underrated LGBTQ+ spots and recurring
events that don't get enough attention. Drop your suggestions — venues, events,
groups, anything.

Building out tulsagays.com as a community resource and want to make sure I'm not
missing anything.
```

## C. Org Outreach — Email Templates
Send to: OKEQ, PFLAG Tulsa, Dennis R. Neill Equality Center, Tulsa PAC,
          Gilcrease Museum (they ran the UnCrease series), Allied Arts Tulsa.

**Template 1: Ask them to share the signup link**
```
Subject: Free resource for your community — weekly LGBTQ+ event calendar

Hi [name],

We run tulsagays.com, a free weekly LGBTQ+ event guide for Tulsa. Every Monday
we publish every queer-relevant event happening in the metro — community gatherings,
arts events, nightlife, support groups, the works.

We have a free weekly email newsletter at tulsagays.com/newsletter that your
members/followers might find useful. If you're willing to share the link in your
own newsletter or social media, we'd really appreciate it.

We also actively list your events on the site — no submission needed, we track
your calendar automatically. But if there are events you'd like to make sure we
catch, you can submit them at tulsagays.com/submit.

Thanks for everything you do for the community.
— The Tulsa Gays team
```

**Template 2: Ask them to submit events (drives site quality + newsletter content)**
```
Subject: Your events on TulsaGays.com

Hi [name],

We run tulsagays.com, a free community events calendar for LGBTQ+ Tulsa.
Your events are already showing up on the site, but we wanted to make sure
you know you can also submit directly at tulsagays.com/submit — especially
for events that might not show up on the public calendar sites we scrape.

We send a weekly email newsletter to subscribers across Tulsa every Monday.
Submitted events are eligible for featured placement.

No cost, no account needed — just a quick form.

Thanks for all the work you do.
— The Tulsa Gays team
```
