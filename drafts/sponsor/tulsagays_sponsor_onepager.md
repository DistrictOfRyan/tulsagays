# Sponsor the TulsaGays Weekly Guide

*The hyper-local, high-intent way to reach Tulsa's LGBTQ+ community, one warm welcome at a time.*

---

## Who we are

TulsaGays is Tulsa's community-first guide to the best LGBTQ+ and queer-friendly events happening around town every week. We are not a corporate calendar. We are the friend who already knows where everybody's going Friday night and is happy to tell you (with a little sass). Every week we round up what's worth showing up for and put it in front of the people most likely to show up.

When you sponsor the guide, you are not buying an ad. You are putting your name on the thing that helps our people find their people. That kind of goodwill sticks.

## Why a queer-friendly Tulsa business should sponsor

Our audience is small on purpose and loyal because of it. These are real Tulsans actively deciding where to spend their weekend, their dollars, and their loyalty. A "brought to you by" line from a community guide reads as a recommendation, not an interruption. For a local bar, restaurant, salon, shop, or service, that is the warmest introduction you can get, and it lands with exactly the neighbors you want walking through your door.

We would rather be straight with you than inflate a single figure. Here is what the weekly guide actually touches:

- **Website:** tulsagays.com, roughly 150 monthly users and climbing
- **Instagram:** @tulsagays, a 9-slide events carousel every week
- **Facebook Page:** the same weekly carousel to our Tulsa Gays Page followers
- **Facebook groups:** shared into around 16 local Tulsa and Oklahoma community groups (7 live now, about 9 more pending admin approval)
- **Email newsletter:** the weekly guide delivered straight to subscriber inboxes

The pitch is not "millions of impressions." The pitch is the opposite. This is small, local, and high-intent. Every person who sees your name is in Tulsa, is engaged with the community, and is deciding where to go this week. That is the audience most local advertising never reaches.

1. **Pick a tier** (or tell us your budget and we will shape something that fits).

4. **You show up in the next guide.** That is it. No long contracts, no fine print, no corporate runaround.
