## What TulsaGays is
Every Monday we publish a curated, hand-written carousel of the week's LGBTQ+ events
across Tulsa — drag, nightlife, community, arts, faith, the works — to a multi-channel
audience, and the full week lives on tulsagays.com with a searchable, day-by-day calendar.
Anonymous, community-first, no gatekeeping. One voice the scene trusts.

## What a PARTNER gets (free, community)
- Listed in the TulsaGays community directory
- Your events featured in the weekly carousel + website whenever you submit them
- A standing channel to reach engaged local LGBTQ+ folks every week
- Ask in return: a link back to tulsagays.com from your site
