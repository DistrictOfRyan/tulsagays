OKC's queer hub is the **NW 39th Street District** ("The Strip," "39th & Penn," "The Gayborhood"),
NW 39th St one block west of Pennsylvania Ave. About 8 gay bars cluster here plus the District Hotel
(formerly Habana Inn) and the LGBT community resources. Unlike Tulsa, OKC has TWO competing Pride orgs
and TWO Pride events (see SIGNATURE_EVENT).

## SIGNATURE_EVENT candidate

OKC is unusual: there are **two** Pride events run by **two different orgs**. Pick a primary, list both.

1. **Pride on 39th** (recommended primary) - hosted by **OKC Pride Inc.** (501c3 since 2004), in the historic 39th St District. 2026 dates **June 5-7, 2026**. This is the bar-district, community-rooted Pride — the natural anchor for an OKCGays nightlife/events site. [Verified Y]
2. **Oklahoma PrideFest** - hosted by **Oklahoma Pride Alliance** (501c3), late June at **Scissortail Park, downtown OKC**, **June 26-28, 2026**. Larger/downtown festival. [Verified Y]

Recommendation: anchor on **Pride on 39th (OKC Pride Inc.)** since it maps to the bar district the site will cover,
and cross-list Oklahoma PrideFest as a second marquee weekend.
