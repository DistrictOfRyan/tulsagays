Rainbow Harvest: Free Farmer's Market

Saturday, August 15 · 1:00 PM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

Rainbow Harvest turns the Equality Center parking lot into a Saturday farmer's market, and it's free to walk through even if you're just there to look and snack your way down the row. Bring cash for the vendors who don't do cards, because sugar, you do not want to be the one standing there fumbling with Apple Pay while people wait behind you. It's low pressure, outdoors, and easy to duck in and out of if crowds aren't your thing. Get there right at 1 when it opens, the good stuff goes first and the lot isn't packed yet.
