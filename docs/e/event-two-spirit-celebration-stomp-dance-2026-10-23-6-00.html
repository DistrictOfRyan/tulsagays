Two Spirit Celebration: Stomp Dance

Friday, October 23 · 6:00 PM

Cherokee Nation Powwow Grounds (next to)

The Intercultural Partnership Council kicks off its Two Spirit Celebration with a Friday-night Stomp Dance right beside the Cherokee Nation Powwow Grounds in Tahlequah, 6 to 10 PM. This is Two-Spirit tradition held with pride: Native LGBTQ folks and the whole community moving together in the circle, the shell-shakers keeping time, everyone welcome no matter where you come from. It is completely alcohol and drug free, and there is no charge for food or activities. Best-time tip for the shy ones: get there right at 6 while the light is still soft and folks are gathering, stand at the edge of the circle to watch and listen first, and when you feel ready, someone will wave you in, that is exactly how the dance is meant to work. General info at tinyurl.com/ipctahlequah.
