Important Summer Textbook Return Dates

Monday, July 6

OSU-Tulsa

Summer textbook return windows are shorter than the fall and spring semesters. Students only have the first five days of each summer session to return textbooks ordered through the OSU University Store. Summer sessions begin May 18, June 8, July 6 and July 10. Summer bursar charges for textbooks will begin after the first five days of each session. It's a Monday thing, over at OSU-Tulsa.
