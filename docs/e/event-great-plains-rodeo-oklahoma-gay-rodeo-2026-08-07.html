Great Plains Rodeo (Oklahoma Gay Rodeo)

Friday, August 7

Oklahoma City (Great Plains Rodeo Association

The Great Plains Rodeo rolls into Oklahoma City with boots polished, buckles shining, and more queer cowboy swagger per square foot than you'll find anywhere else in this state, honey. This is the Oklahoma Gay Rodeo Association's big event, full of barrel racing, roping, and a community that grew up wrangling cattle and never once apologized for being exactly who they are. It's a road trip, sure, but a short one, and a rodeo hits completely different in person than on a screen. Get to the arena early enough to walk the grounds before events kick off, sugar, that's when you scope out the best seats and meet folks before the stands pack in.
