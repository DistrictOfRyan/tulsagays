TULSA EAGLE COUNTRY NIGHT WITH DJ B/BANISHED

Friday, September 18 · 9:00 PM

Tulsa Eagle

Tulsa Eagle's Country Night puts DJ B/Banished behind the decks at 1338 E 3rd for a 9 PM start, and this is a boot-scooting, line-dancing, two-stepping kind of night, not a stand-by-the-wall kind. Wear something you can actually move in, sugar, because a chair is not in your future once that first song hits. It's the country crowd's answer to a club night, so expect some genuine dancing know-how in the room and don't be shy about asking somebody to show you a step. Best time to have the best time: get on that floor in the first twenty minutes, before the room fills in and the good spots by the rail disappear.
