DRAGNIFICENT! Drag Show

Thursday, June 25 · 9:00 PM

Club Majestic

By 10 PM Thursday night at Club Majestic, the lights drop, the music cranks, and DRAGNIFICENT! delivers exactly what that name promises, honey. This is a full drag show with the kind of performances that make you forget you were tired, that make you stand up, that make you realize you've been holding a twenty this whole time and you don't regret a cent. Doors open at 9 PM so you've got a full hour to find your spot, order your drink, and get comfortable before the queens take the stage. Bring a stack of singles to tip the performers because a queen who works that hard deserves to feel the love in her hands, not just in your applause.
