Spotlight Student Art Exhibition | McFarlin Library

Monday, October 12

McFarlin Library

The Spotlight Student Art Exhibition turns McFarlin Library's halls into a quiet gallery crawl, honey, full of work made by students who are just getting started and already have something to say. It's free, it's low-pressure, and it's the kind of outing that fits into a lunch break or a lazy afternoon without any planning. You don't need to know a thing about art to stand in front of a piece and feel something. Best time to have the best time: go on a weekday afternoon when the library's quiet, so you can actually linger in front of the pieces that catch you instead of shuffling past.
