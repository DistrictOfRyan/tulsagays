Tulsa Pride Festival & Parade

Saturday, October 17 · 3:00 PM

E 6th St & S Frankfort Ave

Tulsa Pride is the main event, Oklahomans for Equality's flagship parade and festival that fills downtown's Tulsa Arts District with floats, drag, vendors, food, and the entire community out and proud. Note that OKEQ moved the big Pride to October, so this, not June, is Tulsa's marquee Pride. The 2026 parade starts at 4 p.m., and road construction around the Equality Center moves the festival to East 6th Street and South Frankfort Avenue this year. Best-time tip: stake out a parade spot early, then flow into the festival, wear sunscreen and comfortable shoes, and pace yourself because this one goes all day.
