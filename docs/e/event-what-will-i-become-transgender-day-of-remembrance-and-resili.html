What Will I Become? Transgender Day of Remembrance and Resilience Screening

Friday, November 20 · 7:00 PM

Oklahoma City / Tulsa area

Freedom Oklahoma and the Oklahoma Film Exchange are bringing "What Will I Become?" to the Tulsa area for Transgender Day of Remembrance and Resilience, a screening built to honor both grief and grit. This isn't a night to sit home scrolling through headlines, it's a night to be physically present with people who understand exactly why this day matters. The film carries real weight, so come ready to feel something alongside a room full of people doing the same. Arrive a little early to settle in before the lights go down, and stay afterward if there's space to talk, because that's often where the real connection happens.
