Exploring Fiber Arts: Crochet Made Easy

Thursday, October 8

Living Arts of Tulsa

Guided by Carolyn Argo, attendees will learn the basics of crochet, learning the stitches and terminology that form the base of most crochet patterns. They will create their own swatch to take home, alongside instructions on how to create their first big project. From basic stitches to reading patterns, this class will prepare participants to create their own crochet creations! Best for beginners, but open to all skill levels! For ages 14-Adult. It's a Thursday thing, over at Living Arts of Tulsa. Bring your whole personality. That's the only ticket this town really checks.
