Equality Business Alliance Networking Mixer

Thursday, August 27 · 6:00 PM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

Once a month, the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center turns into a room full of LGBTQ+ business folks trading cards, ideas, and the occasional referral, and it's exactly as low-pressure as it sounds. You don't need a pitch deck, sugar, just a smile and a business card (or your phone, we're modern). It's an hour and a half, so nobody's trapped making small talk till midnight. Best time to work the room: get there by 6:15, grab a name tag, and post up near the snack table, that's where the conversations start themselves.
