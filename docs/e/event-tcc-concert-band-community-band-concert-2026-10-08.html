TCC Concert Band & Community Band Concert

Thursday, October 8

Tulsa Community College

Show up for the opener, sugar, the Concert Band and Community Band concert always has one act that sneaks up and steals the whole night. You get two ensembles worth of sound, a full range from community players to the sharper student lineup, all in one sitting. It's the kind of low pressure night where you can just sit back, listen, and not have to make a single bit of small talk if you don't want to. Best time to get there is a little early so you can post up near the back by the doors, close enough to the bar for an easy refill and with a clear sightline to the whole stage.
