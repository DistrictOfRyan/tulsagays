SHUT UP & WRITE (Foolish Things)

Saturday, August 1 · 10:30 AM

Foolish Things Coffee Company

Shut Up & Write!® at Foolish Things SUAW hosts free in-person and online writing events for writers all over the world. Our goal is to ensure that every writer, regardless of genre or skill level, has access to the community, accountability, and resources they need to be successful in their personal writing goals. Our events use our proven formula: a few minutes of introductions, an hour of focused writing, and a final check-in to share the hour’s progress. What to Expect: RSVP and please arrive 10 to 15 minutes early to get settled at an in-person event. Our host will lead introductions and then everyone will write for a quiet, focused period of time. Afterward, we’ll have an optional time to chat and get to know each other. Members often talk about their successes and challenges as writers. Our events are a safe space for writers to work on their craft. No one will read or critique your writing. It's a Saturday thing, around 10:30 AM, over at Foolish Things Coffee Company. Dress how it makes you feel, roll in a little early, and thank yourself later, sugar.
