Drag Bingo

Sunday, July 12

You get a bingo card. A queen calls the numbers between show-stopping drag performances. Money raised goes to Adonia, an LGBTQ+ youth organization doing real work in Tulsa. This is the most structurally perfect evening this city produces on a regular basis, and I mean that without any exaggeration: a format that is exciting enough to hold the room, performers who are genuinely committed to the work, and a fundraising purpose that means your Saturday night is also doing something meaningful in the world. The rhythm is drag number, bingo round, drag number, bingo round, and you repeat that until you have screamed BINGO or lost your voice cheering for someone's number, whichever comes first and both are honestly a win. Buy a card. Tip your queen generously because she is working. Scream when you win. You're having fun and funding something that matters, and that combination does not come around often enough.
