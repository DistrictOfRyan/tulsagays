Parent & Family Weekend Check In

Saturday, September 19 · 3:00 PM

Collins Hall

Stop by Collins Hall to check in, enter the drawing for a UTulsa swag bag, and pick up a special discount card to use at participating Tulsa-based McNellie’s Group restaurants. Families can also pick up complimentary family buttons and stickers to show their UTulsa pride. It's a Saturday thing, around 3:00 PM, over at Collins Hall. Put on the look, bring a friend or bring your nerve, and let Tulsa show you a good time.
