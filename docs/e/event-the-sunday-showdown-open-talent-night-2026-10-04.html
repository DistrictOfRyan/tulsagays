The Sunday Showdown! Open Talent Night

Sunday, October 4

Club Majestic

The Sunday Showdown rolls into Club Majestic with Shanel Sterling emceeing an open talent night, featuring Scrappy Legacy and Londenn Davenport Raine bringing the kind of performances that make you gasp out loud. Anybody brave enough can sign up to hit the stage, so the lineup swings from polished numbers to glorious chaos, and honestly both are a good time. Doors open at 9 but the show doesn't kick off till 11, so pace your drinks and your energy like the night owl you're about to become. Grab a seat close to the stage before showtime, that's where you catch every reaction shot on the judges' faces.
