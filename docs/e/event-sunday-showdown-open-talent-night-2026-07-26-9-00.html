Sunday Showdown Open Talent Night

Sunday, July 26 · 9:00 PM

Club Majestic

Sunday Showdown Open Talent Night at Club Majestic is the kind of night where amateur nerves turn into showstopping moments, and you get to be part of the crowd that makes it happen. Pull up to the bar, order something with a little sparkle in it, and say hello to whoever's beside you like y'all go way back, darling, that's just how Majestic works. The gap between doors and showtime is built for exactly that kind of easy mingling, no pressure, just vibes and a good soundtrack. Best time to go: arrive right at doors so you snag a spot near the stage before the 11pm crowd surge, that's where the reactions (and the tips) hit hardest.
