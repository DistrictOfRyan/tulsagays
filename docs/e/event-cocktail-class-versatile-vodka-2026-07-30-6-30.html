Cocktail Class: Versatile Vodka

Thursday, July 30 · 6:30 PM

The Vault

Vodka’s not basic - it’s the base. Join us July 30th for four bold cocktails that prove vodka is anything but boring. Four vodka cocktails, lite bites, clean lines. July 30 at The Vault. $40 per person, 21+. Grab your seat. Vodka takes the spotlight in this curated cocktail class built around its range & flexibility. You’ll sip four inventive drinks that show how this spirit can shift from subtle to bold, bright to unexpected, all in one night. Lite bites are served along the way, & our full dinner menu is available if you’d like to stay awhile. Event Details: • Arrival: The dining room opens 30 minutes before the class. Come early, get settled, & feel free to order dinner, appetizers, or desserts from The Vault’s full menu at an additional cost. • Experience: Your instructor will demonstrate each cocktail, sharing techniques, flavor pairings, & a few surprises along the way. Sip & enjoy each round as the class unfolds. • Cost: $40 per person. Must be 21+ to attend. Please note: Due to Oklahoma liquor laws, cocktails will be prepared by your instructor. Tickets are non-refundable within two weeks of the event but may be transferred to another guest. Specialty ingredients are ordered in advance based on ticket count. It's a Thursday thing, around 6:30 PM, over at The Vault. Bring your whole personality. That's the only ticket this town really checks.
