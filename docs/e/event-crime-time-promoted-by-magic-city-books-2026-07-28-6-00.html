Crime Time (Promoted by Magic City Books)

Tuesday, July 28 · 6:00 PM

Magic City Books

Crime Time, hosted at Magic City Books, is for the ones who would much rather unpack a real case or a twisty plot than stand around swapping small talk. It's a built-in conversation starter for anyone who loves true crime, mystery, or just a good "wait, but WHY did they do that" debate. You get instant common ground with strangers over shared morbid curiosity, which honestly makes the whole night easier. Get there a few minutes early and grab a seat near the discussion circle so you're in the thick of the theories from the first minute.
