MONDAY MOVIE NIGHT

Monday, October 5 · 7:00 PM

Tulsa Eagle

Free movie night at one of Tulsa's most beloved gay bars. Tonight it's a punk rock documentary, which means you are going to walk in knowing approximately nothing about the Minutemen and walk out with a new favorite band and three strong opinions about the 1980s underground music scene that you will be voicing to people for weeks. The bar is full of people who get it. The drinks are real. The movie costs you nothing. You were going to watch something on your couch alone tonight anyway, so watch it here, in a room full of actual humans who will be having the same experience right alongside you, which is a fundamentally different thing. Doors open early. Show up when you feel like it. Let yourself be surprised by what a good movie and a good bar can do to a Monday night.
