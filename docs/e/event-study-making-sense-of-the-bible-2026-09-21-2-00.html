Study: Making Sense of the Bible

Monday, September 21 · 2:00 PM

Wesley Foundation

We live in a culture split over how to read Scripture. And even though it seems like we’re fighting over how to understand specific social issues, it’s really about how we understand the Bible itself. Did God really command Moses to put gay people to death? Did Jesus really teach that everyone who is not a Christian will be assigned to hell? Why would Paul command women to “keep silent in the church?” Were Adam and Eve real people? Is the book of Revelation really about the end times?
