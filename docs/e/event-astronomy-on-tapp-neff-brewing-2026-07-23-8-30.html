Astronomy on Tapp (Neff brewing)

Thursday, July 23 · 8:30 PM

NEFF Brewing

The Astronomy Club of Tulsa is joining with NEFF Brewing in downtown Tulsa to start a new program called "Astronomy on Tap". This is a program targeted towards the adult only crowd (must be 18 year of age) in a relaxed setting to share information about the night skies. The Astronomy Club of Tulsa will present a short program about an astronomy topic and be available to answer questions about astronomy and our club. Due to the location in downtown Tulsa the night skies may offer challenges for telescope viewing but we will have some telescopes setup, weather permitting. NEFF Brewing offers a selection of local craft brews along with a great dinner menu and desserts.
