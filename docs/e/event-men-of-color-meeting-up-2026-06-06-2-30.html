Men of Color Meeting Up

Saturday, June 6 · 2:30 PM

Chick-fil-A

Men of Color Meeting Up is an easy, come-as-you-are gathering at the Chick-fil-A on E 13th St, a space for fathers, brothers, and uncles to connect, network, and grow together. No RSVP, no formality, just show up and find your people, brand new to Tulsa or a lifelong local alike. If you have been hunting for a real friend group, a network, or a barbershop-style circle to belong to, this is built for exactly that. The welcome is genuine and the door is wide open. Just show up around 2:30, no sign-up needed, and let the conversation find its own rhythm.
