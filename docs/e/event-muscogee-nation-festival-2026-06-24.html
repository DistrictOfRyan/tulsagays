Muscogee Nation Festival

Wednesday, June 24

Claude Cox Omniplex

The drums and the color and the whole beautiful weight of Muscogee Nation culture lands at the Claude Cox Omniplex today for the Muscogee Nation Festival. This is the kind of event where you walk in knowing almost nothing and walk out with a whole new layer of appreciation for the land you're living on, sugar. Cultural festivals like this bring together food, artisans, demonstrations, and performances you genuinely cannot find anywhere else under one roof. Go with an open mind and an empty stomach, and take your time at every vendor booth because that's where the real conversations happen.
