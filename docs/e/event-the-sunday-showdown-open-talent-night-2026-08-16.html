The Sunday Showdown! Open Talent Night

Sunday, August 16

Club Majestic

The Sunday Showdown at Club Majestic is open talent night, doors at 9, show at 11, hosted by Shanel Sterling and friends spotlighting the up-and-comers who might be your new favorite performer by the time the lights come up. It's a later night, sure, but that's exactly what makes it feel like a secret, the crowd that stays this late is the crowd that's genuinely there for the talent. You get to watch someone's big moment happen in real time. Best time to have the best time: grab a seat near the stage before 11, this is the show where the front row actually matters.
