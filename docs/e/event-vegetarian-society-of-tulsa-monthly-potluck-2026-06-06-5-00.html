Vegetarian Society of Tulsa Monthly Potluck

Saturday, June 6 · 5:00 PM

Martin East Regional Library

The Vegetarian Society of Tulsa Monthly Potluck at Martin East Regional Library is the group's most beloved gathering, a spread of plant-based dishes and a roomful of like-minded folks happy to talk food and life. You bring a vegan or vegetarian dish to share, and in return you get a sampler plate and a whole table of new acquaintances. A potluck is a stealthy great option for the shy, the food gives everyone an instant, easy thing to talk about. No awkward openers required. Come a little hungry, bring your dish, and arrive near the 5 PM start so you can set up and chat before the line forms.
