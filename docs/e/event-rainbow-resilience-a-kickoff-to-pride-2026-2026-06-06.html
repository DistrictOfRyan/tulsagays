RAINBOW RESILIENCE: A Kickoff to PRIDE 2026

Saturday, June 6

Dennis R Neill Equality Center

RAINBOW RESILIENCE is the kickoff to PRIDE 2026, and it lights the fuse on the season right where the community heart beats, the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center. It is the big, joyful gathering of the year, the moment the whole crew comes together to celebrate how far we have come and how loudly we plan to show up. If you have ever wanted one easy doorway into Tulsa's LGBTQIA+ community, a Pride kickoff is it, everyone is here and everyone is glad you came. Walk in, say hi to one friendly face, and arrive on the early side so you can take in the room before it fills with the celebration.
