Queer Crafters Club

Thursday, November 19 · 6:00 PM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center turns into a cozy mess of glue sticks, yarn, and good company every time this club meets, and this Thursday is no different. Nobody here is checking your credentials, sugar, so if your crafting skills peaked at a kindergarten macaroni necklace, you are exactly who this is for. It's quiet, it's low pressure, and it's the kind of room where conversation happens sideways while your hands stay busy. Get there right at 6pm, grab a seat at the table instead of the couch, and let whoever's next to you show you what they're working on, that's how you make a friend without having to try.
