Tulsa Gays · LGBTQ+ Event

Ify Nwadiwe

Sunday, October 25 · 4:00 PM

Bricktown Comedy Club Tulsa

Ify Nwadiwe is bringing his stand-up to Bricktown Comedy Club, and this is the kind of room built for people who like to watch first and laugh their way into joining in. You get to sit in the dark with a drink, let somebody funny do all the talking, and clap when you feel like it, no small talk required. It is a real reason to get off the couch that asks nothing of you but showing up and laughing. Best time to grab your seat is a little before 4, so you land somewhere in the middle of the room where the laughs hit hardest but nobody is looking right at you.
