Thank You, Veterans

Wednesday, November 11 · 9:00 AM

Philbrook Museum of Art

Thank You, Veterans brings its Philbrook show back for 2026, and this one asks nothing of you but your presence. No performance pressure, no dress code anxiety, just quiet galleries and a chance to pay respect at your own pace. It's the kind of outing that fits even your laziest Wednesday self, darling, because you can be in and out in under an hour. Best time to go: swing by right at 9am opening when the museum is hushed and you'll have the exhibit practically to yourself.
