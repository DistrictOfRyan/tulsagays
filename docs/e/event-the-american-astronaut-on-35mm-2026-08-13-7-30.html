The American Astronaut on 35mm

Thursday, August 13 · 7:30 PM

Circle Cinema

Circle Cinema is running The American Astronaut on real 35mm film, the way this gloriously weird cult musical space-western was always meant to be seen. This one-night-only screening is your shot at a movie that doesn't stream anywhere near this good, sugar, all grain and glow and celluloid crackle. It's strange, it's funny, it's got songs you didn't know you needed, and it deserves an audience that gets it. Best time tip: arrive by 7:15 for a good seat, the film print crowd fills in fast and you want to be settled before the lights go down.
