Tulsa Shakespeare Co. presents Hamlet

Sunday, November 1 · 3:00 PM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

Tulsa Shakespeare Co. Is staging the big one, sugar, skulls, swordplay, and the whole doomed Danish mess, right at the Equality Center on Sunday afternoon. This is matinee theater done right: no late night, no fumbling for parking in the dark, just two hours of gorgeous language in a room that gasps together during the ghost scenes. Hamlet never gets old because somebody always finds a new way to make "to be or not to be" land right in your chest, honey. Arrive by 2:45 to grab a seat with a clear sightline so you catch every raised eyebrow before the curtain call.
