Queering Body Image Group

Wednesday, September 23 · 4:00 PM

MAIN HALL

This group provides safe space for LGBTQ+ individuals to connect with others, share their experiences, and provide and receive support, as well as explore body-image, self-esteem, and identity. It aims to build community, strengthen knowledge of local resources, and process the impact of systems of oppressionon mental wellbeing. The group is free to attend, meets in-person and is open to LGBTQ+ adults. Registration for Al Carlozzi Center for Counseling groups is required. REGISTER HERE. It's a Wednesday thing, around 4:00 PM, over at MAIN HALL. Bring your whole personality. That's the only ticket this town really checks.
