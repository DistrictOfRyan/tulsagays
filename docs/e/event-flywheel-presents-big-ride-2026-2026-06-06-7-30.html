Flywheel Presents: Big Ride 2026

Saturday, June 6 · 7:30 PM

Flywheel Field (Walk right up from the Cains through the overpass to 501 N. Main)

Flywheel Presents: Big Ride 2026 turns downtown Tulsa into a one-day celebration of music, food, art, and tech, all energy and motion at Flywheel Field. The instruction from the organizers is simple, sit back, relax, and enjoy the ride, and the lineup of sights and sounds delivers on it. Big festival-style events like this are great even when you are flying solo, there is always something to watch and the crowd carries its own momentum. Just walk up from the Cains through the overpass to 501 N. Main. It is a 21+ event, so bring your ID, go hungry, wear comfortable shoes, and let the night unfold from the 7:30 start.
