DRAGNIFICENT! at Club Majestic

Thursday, August 6

DRAGNIFICENT! Is Club Majestic's weekly drag showcase, hosted by the incomparable Shanel Sterling with a rotating lineup that keeps the numbers fresh week after week. It's the kind of night where you can just sit back and let the performers do the emotional labor, no small talk required until you're ready for it. Bring a few singles for the tip rail, sugar, that's how you tell a queen she landed the number. Get there early enough to grab a seat with a real sightline, the good spots near the stage fill up fast once Shanel steps up to the mic.
