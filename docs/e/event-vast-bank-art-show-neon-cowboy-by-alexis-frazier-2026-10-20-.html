Vast Bank Art Show: Neon Cowboy by Alexis Frazier,

Tuesday, October 20 · 7:30 AM

Alexis Frazier's Neon Cowboy show turns Vast Bank's gallery into a glowing, buzzing little rodeo of color, the kind of art that looks like it's humming even when it's standing still. This is the good kind of art opening, sugar, the one where you can sip a free glass of wine and just stare at something gorgeous without anybody quizzing you on brushstrokes. It's a quiet, pretty way to ease into being out among people, no performance required. Swing in right at 7:30 when the lights are freshest and the crowd's still thin enough to actually get up close to the glow.
