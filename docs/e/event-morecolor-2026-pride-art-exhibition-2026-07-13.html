MOREcolor 2026: Pride Art Exhibition

Monday, July 13

MOREcolor 2026 turns Pride season into a proper art crawl, honey, walls covered edge to edge with paintings, photography, and mixed media from 2SLGBTQ+ artists across the region. This isn't just a look-don't-touch situation either, sugar, everything on those walls is for sale if a piece speaks to you. Come for the color, stay for the conversations with artists who'll happily tell you the story behind every brushstroke. Get there in the first hour so you can actually hear yourself think before the room fills in, and bring a little cash or a card if something catches your eye, because the good pieces don't linger.
