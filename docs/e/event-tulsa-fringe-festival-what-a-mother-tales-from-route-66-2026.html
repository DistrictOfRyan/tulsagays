Tulsa Fringe Festival: What a Mother-Tales from Route 66

Thursday, July 23 · 6:30 PM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

What a Mother: Tales from Route 66 is a storytelling show tucked into Fringe Fest at the Equality Center, all narrative and heart, no audience participation required. This one is built for the quiet ones, honey, the folks who would rather sink into a seat and let a good story wash over them than work a room. You get to just sit there and feel things, which is frankly underrated as a night out. Slip in a few minutes before 6:30, grab a seat toward the middle where the sound carries best, and let the tale do the rest.
