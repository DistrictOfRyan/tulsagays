Happy Hour with Free Pool at Caz’s Pub

Monday, July 20 · 2:00 PM

Caz's Pub

Caz's Pub is throwing open the doors for happy hour with free pool from 2 to 6, which means cheap fun and an easy excuse to strike up a conversation over who's up next on the table. The move here is simple, darling, find someone in a great outfit, tell them so, and let the small talk build from there, pool halls are basically built for exactly that kind of opener. Nobody's watching the clock at a happy hour like this, so you can ease in slow and still catch the whole thing. Best time to go: swing by right around 2 when the crowd's thin and the table's open, easiest way to actually get a game in and meet someone before the room fills up.
