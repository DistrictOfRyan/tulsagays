ACT Pride Table

Saturday, October 17 · 9:00 AM

The ACT Pride Table is about as gentle an entry into Pride weekend as it gets, a simple table set up for you to stop by, grab some info, and chat for as long or as little as you want. There's no commitment here, no sitting through a program, just a friendly face and a reason to be out in the world for five minutes. It's the perfect warm-up if big crowds make you want to stay home. Swing by whenever suits you, say hello, and let that one small stop be your whole Pride outing for the day if that's all you've got in you.
