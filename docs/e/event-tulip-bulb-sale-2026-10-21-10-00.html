Tulip Bulb Sale

Wednesday, October 21 · 10:00 AM

Philbrook Museum of Art

Philbrook's Tulip Bulb Sale means acres of garden and blooms you can wander through without a single obligation to chat anybody up. It's the kind of outing where being quiet is basically the assignment, just you, some sunshine, and more tulips than you know what to do with. Bring a tote bag, because bulbs are the whole point and you will want to take some home. Arrive right at 10 when it opens, the selection's freshest and the paths are still peaceful before the crowds wander in.
