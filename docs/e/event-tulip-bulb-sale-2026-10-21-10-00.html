Tulip Bulb Sale

Wednesday, October 21 · 10:00 AM

Philbrook Museum of Art

Philbrook turns its grounds into a tulip bulb sale, which is really just an excuse to wander a gorgeous museum property surrounded by color and no obligation to perform charm for anybody. You show up, you look at flowers, you leave happier, that is the whole transaction, sugar. Morning light in a garden full of tulips is the kind of quiet joy that costs you nothing but showing up. Get there right at 10am opening so you've got the paths to yourself before the crowds roll in, and bring a tote, because you will absolutely leave with more bulbs than you planned on.
