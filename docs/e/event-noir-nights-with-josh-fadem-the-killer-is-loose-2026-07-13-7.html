Noir Nights with Josh Fadem: The Killer Is Loose

Monday, July 13 · 7:00 PM

Circle Cinema

Noir Nights with Josh Fadem keeps rolling at Circle Cinema, and this month's pick is The Killer Is Loose, a proper slice of classic noir shown exactly the way it was meant to be seen, on a real screen in a real theater full of people who love this stuff as much as you do. Josh brings the kind of introduction that makes you appreciate the shadows and double-crosses even more before the lights go down. It's a low-key night out, sugar, dark room, good film, no small talk required unless you want it. Get there by 6:45 so you can grab popcorn and a middle seat before the previews start, that's prime real estate for a film like this.
