September OK So: KARMA

Friday, September 11

Living Arts of Tulsa

Once a month we come together and hear people tell TRUE (funny, serious, sad, harrowing, interesting) stories about their lives. HOW DOES A STORY SLAM WORK? Every event has a theme, and everyone in the audience is invited to share a personal story related to the theme. This month the theme is KARMA. Plenty of stories can be molded to fit this theme, so BE BRAVE and come and put your name in the hat! If you have a story you want to tell, leave us your name at the door. Every It's a Friday thing, over at Living Arts of Tulsa. Bring your whole personality. That's the only ticket this town really checks.
