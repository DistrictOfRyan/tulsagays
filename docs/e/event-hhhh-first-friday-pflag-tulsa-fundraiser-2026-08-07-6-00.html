HHHH First Friday: PFLAG Tulsa Fundraiser

Friday, August 7 · 6:00 PM

Courtyard Downtown

August's Homo Hotel Happy Hour lands at the Courtyard Downtown on Boston Ave, 6 to 8 p.m., and it is a fundraiser for PFLAG Tulsa, the folks who have been showing up for 2SLGBTQIA+ Tulsans and their families every month for years. First Fridays at HHHH are the easiest social on the calendar: a hotel bar full of friendly faces, no dress code, no agenda, come alone and you will not stay alone long. This one comes with a bonus: the money goes somewhere that matters.
