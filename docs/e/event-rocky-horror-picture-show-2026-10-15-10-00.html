Rocky Horror Picture Show

Thursday, October 15 · 10:00 PM

Club Majestic

Rocky Horror Picture Show takes over Club Majestic, Tulsa's flagship LGBTQ+ nightclub downtown, for a night built on fishnets, call-backs, and a crowd that knows every line before it's spoken. This isn't a sit-quietly-in-the-dark kind of show, it's participation sport, and Club Majestic's downtown stage was made for exactly this kind of glorious chaos. Check @clubmajestictulsa on Instagram beforehand for the latest details so you walk in knowing the lineup and the dress code mood. Arrive early enough to find your people before the Time Warp starts, that's when the room goes from fun to unforgettable, and let the regulars pull you into the callbacks, nobody's grading you on it.
