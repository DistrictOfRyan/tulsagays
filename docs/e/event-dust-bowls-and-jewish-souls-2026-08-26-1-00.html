Dust Bowls and Jewish Souls

Wednesday, August 26 · 1:00 PM

Circle Cinema

Dust Bowls and Jewish Souls keeps its daily afternoon run going at Circle Cinema, with today's screening at 1:00pm giving you yet another easy window to catch it if the timing hasn't lined up yet. The film traces Jewish families and communities through Oklahoma's Dust Bowl years, a piece of the state's story that rarely gets told this directly, darling. A single-screen indie theater like Circle Cinema makes for a genuinely comfortable place to go it alone, the vibe is more "film lover" than "big crowd." Early afternoon on a weekday is your best bet for an emptier house, so grab a seat wherever you like and settle in.
