Workshop with 108 exhibiting artist Aaron Jones at the TCC McKeon Center for Creativity, Free Event

Monday, August 17 · 5:30 PM

108 Contemporary

This free workshop puts you in the room with Aaron Jones, one of the exhibiting artists currently showing at 108 Contemporary, and workshops like this are where you actually get to ask a working artist how they do what they do instead of just admiring it from across a gallery. It's low stakes and low cost (free, darling, so no excuse there), which makes it the easiest kind of night out for anyone who'd rather make something than mingle over cocktails. Best time to have the best time: get to the TCC McKeon Center for Creativity a few minutes early to grab a spot near the front, and don't worry about your art skills, honey, workshops like this are built for beginners who just want to try.
