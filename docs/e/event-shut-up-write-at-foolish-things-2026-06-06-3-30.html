Shut Up & Write!® at Foolish Things

Saturday, June 6 · 3:30 PM

Foolish Things Coffee

Shut Up & Write!® at Foolish Things Coffee is a free, in-person writing session with a wonderfully simple premise, everyone gathers, goes quiet, and writes side by side. Any genre, any skill level, you bring your own project and the group supplies the focus and gentle accountability that solo sessions so often lack. It is tailor-made for introverts, the silence is the social contract, so there is zero pressure to chat until the sprint wraps. Real company, no draining small talk. Bring your laptop or notebook and arrive by the 3:30 start so you can grab a coffee and settle in before the writing begins.
