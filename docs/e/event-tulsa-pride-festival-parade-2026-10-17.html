Tulsa Pride Festival & Parade

Saturday, October 17

E 6th St & S Frankfort Ave

Oklahomans for Equality takes over downtown for the Tulsa Pride Festival and Parade, and honey, it is the whole shebang: a parade route down 6th and Frankfort, vendor booths stacked three deep, drag queens working every inch of stage, and music loud enough to feel in your chest. This is the one day a year Tulsa turns a city block into glitter and noise just for you. Grab a spot along the parade route early if you want a good view, because the crowd packs in fast once the floats start rolling, and bring cash for the vendor tents since that's where the best handmade stuff goes first.
