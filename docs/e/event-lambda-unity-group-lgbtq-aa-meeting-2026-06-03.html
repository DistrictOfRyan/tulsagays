Lambda Unity Group (LGBTQ+ AA Meeting)

Wednesday, June 3

Fellowship Congregational Church

Recovery is hard enough without having to leave part of yourself at the door, which is why an LGBTQ+ specific meeting matters so much. Lambda Unity Group gathers at Fellowship Congregational Church as an AA meeting by and for the queer community, where the shared understanding runs deeper than the twelve steps alone. The people in that room get the particular weight you are carrying, and they hold it with you without a word of judgment. It is steady, warm, and fiercely on your side. Check the current listing to confirm the time before you head out, and if walking in feels heavy, give yourself permission to sit near the back and just be present until you are ready for more.
