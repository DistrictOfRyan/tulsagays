Drag Brunch : aug. 8th - drag university / back to school

Saturday, August 8 · 11:00 AM

Elote Cafe & Catering

Elote Cafe & Catering is turning brunch into Drag University this Saturday, a back-to-school themed drag brunch with all the sass a syllabus never had. You'll get performances woven right between bites of brunch, the kind of show where the professors definitely didn't get their looks approved by any dean. The bit is simple and delicious: can you finish your mimosa before the first lip sync hits? Get there right at 11 AM sharp so you've got a full drink in hand before class starts, that's the only way to actually win the pop quiz.
