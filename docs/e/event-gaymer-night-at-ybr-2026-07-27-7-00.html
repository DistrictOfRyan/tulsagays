Gaymer Night at YBR

Monday, July 27 · 7:00 PM

Yellow Brick Road

Yellow Brick Road is Tulsa's only lesbian bar and it turns into a proper arcade hangout tonight, screens glowing, controllers clacking, that specific joy of button-mashing next to people who get it. This is the low-stakes version of going out, darling, you don't have to talk if you don't want to, you just have to show up and play. Somebody behind that bar already knows your order, which takes the whole "what do I even say" panic off the table. Best time to have it: swing in right at 7 while the games are still open and claim your spot before the good machine gets taken.
