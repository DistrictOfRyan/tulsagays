Make Em Laugh Oklahoma

Friday, October 23 · 6:00 PM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

Baby, Make Em Laugh Oklahoma is comedy the way it should feel, a friendly room at the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center where nobody's judging how loud you laugh or whether you laugh at all. It's a starter social kind of night, easy to slip into, easy to slip out of if you need a breather. Local comedians working a room that already likes them is a different, warmer kind of funny than a big club show. Best time to go: get there by 5:45 so you can grab a seat before the room fills in, middle or back rows if you're feeling shy, front rows get called on more.
