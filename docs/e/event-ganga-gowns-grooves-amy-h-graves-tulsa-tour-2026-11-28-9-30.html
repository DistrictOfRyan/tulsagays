Ganga, Gowns & Grooves (Amy H. Graves Tulsa Tour)

Saturday, November 28 · 9:30 PM

Tulsa House of Drag

Amy H. Graves brings her Tulsa Tour stop to Tulsa House of Drag with gowns, grooves, and presumably a little ganga-adjacent cheekiness in the name alone, which tells you this crowd knows how to have fun without taking itself too seriously. Nobody's checking your plus-one status at the door, honey, solo is a completely normal way to walk into a drag show. You get the glamour, the music, and the chance to just watch the show without managing a group text about where to sit. Get there a little before 9:30 so you can grab a spot with a clear sightline to the stage before the room fills in.
