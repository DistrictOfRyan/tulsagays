Food Truck Wednesdays (Guthrie Green)

Wednesday, June 24 · 11:00 AM

Guthrie Green

The smell of whatever's sizzling in the nearest food truck is basically a siren call the moment you step onto Guthrie Green at 11 AM Wednesday. Food Truck Wednesdays is a weekly lunchtime tradition where Tulsa's rolling kitchens park up and the people actually come out, and baby, it is the most effortless midday break you'll take all week. Grab your food, claim a patch of grass, let the sun do its thing, and remember that Wednesday can genuinely feel good. Get there right at 11 before the lunch rush peaks so you can browse all your options without a line breathing down your neck.
