Black Burns Fast - Twisted Arts Film Fest

Saturday, September 26 · 3:00 PM

Circle Cinema

This special Twisted Arts Film Fest screening at Circle Cinema spotlights Black Burns Fast, directed by Sandulela Asa, a film built around stories that don't often get this kind of spotlight in Tulsa. It's pay-what-you-can, so cost never has to be the thing standing between you and a powerful afternoon at the movies. Circle Cinema's intimate seating means every screening here feels like a shared experience, not just a solo watch in the dark. Get there around 2:30 to grab a good seat and catch any pre-film introduction, and stay through the credits, festival screenings like this often save the best conversation for after.
