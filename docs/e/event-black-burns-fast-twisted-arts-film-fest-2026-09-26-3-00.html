Black Burns Fast - Twisted Arts Film Fest

Saturday, September 26 · 3:00 PM

Circle Cinema

Black Burns Fast screens as part of the Twisted Arts Film Fest at Circle Cinema, and it's pay-what-you-can, so the only real barrier between you and an afternoon of vision is walking through the door. Circle Cinema is the kind of spot that makes even a solo movie trip feel like an occasion, dim lights, good seats, and a screen worth staring at. A 3pm start means you've still got your whole Saturday night ahead of you afterward. Get there a little early to grab a good seat and browse the concession counter, that pay-what-you-can price leaves a little room in the budget for popcorn, darling.
