PFLAG Tulsa Monthly Meeting

Saturday, June 6 · 7:00 PM

110 S Hartford Ave

The PFLAG Tulsa Monthly Meeting gathers at 110 S Hartford Ave at 7 PM, and it is one of the most quietly powerful rooms in the city, full of families, allies, and LGBTQIA+ folks supporting one another. Looking for acceptance you did not get elsewhere, or hoping to be the steady presence for someone else, this is where that happens. The people in that room are your people, and you only find that out by going. It is the kind of meeting you leave feeling lighter than when you walked in. Put it on your calendar now, arrive right at 7, and sit near the back if nerves are running high, no one will rush you.
