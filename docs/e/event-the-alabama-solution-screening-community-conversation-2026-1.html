THE ALABAMA SOLUTION: SCREENING & COMMUNITY CONVERSATION

Sunday, November 8 · 1:00 PM

Flagship / Tulsa Artist Fellowship

The Alabama Solution is a documentary about incarceration, screening free at the Flagship space inside Tulsa Artist Fellowship, followed by a conversation with Oklahoma advocates working on these exact issues. This is the kind of afternoon that actually changes how you see the system, not just a screening you clap for and forget. Staying for the conversation afterward is where the real value is, that's your chance to ask the advocates something you've always wondered. Sugar, get there a little before 1pm so you're settled before it starts, and stick around through the Q&A even if you're tempted to slip out early, that's the part worth being there for.
