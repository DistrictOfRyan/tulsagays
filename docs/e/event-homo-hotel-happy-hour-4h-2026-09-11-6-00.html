Homo Hotel Happy Hour (4H)

Friday, September 11 · 6:00 PM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

Homo Hotel Happy Hour turns the Equality Center into a proper mixer, the kind where everyone's dressed a little intentionally and hoping somebody notices. The whole point is talking to people you don't know yet, so leave the phone in your pocket and let yourself be a little bold. One good compliment on somebody's shoes or shirt is basically a magic password into a conversation. Best time to have the best time: arrive right at 6 while the room's still filling in and easier to circulate, pick one person with a standout outfit, and open with the compliment before you lose your nerve.
