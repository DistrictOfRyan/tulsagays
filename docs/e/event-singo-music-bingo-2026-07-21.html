Singo! Music Bingo

Tuesday, July 21

Broken Arrow Brewing Company

Singo! Music Bingo turns Broken Arrow Brewing Company into a room full of strangers squinting at their cards, trying to place that one song from three notes in. You don't have to carry a tune or even make small talk, honey, just recognize a hook and mark your square, that's the whole game. It's low pressure flirting disguised as trivia, the kind of night where eye contact happens because someone else got Bingo first. Get there early enough to grab a table near the speakers and the host, that's where the best rounds (and the best people watching) happen.
