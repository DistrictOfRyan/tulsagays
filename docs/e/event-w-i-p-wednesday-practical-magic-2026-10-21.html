W.I.P. Wednesday: Practical Magic

Wednesday, October 21

Living Arts of Tulsa

W.I.P. Wednesday turns the Living Arts gallery into a cozy little creative clubhouse, where you bring whatever you're working on and a movie plays softly in the background while everyone just. Makes things, together but not performing for each other. This week's film is Practical Magic, which is basically patron saint programming for anyone who's ever wanted a little witchy courage. There's no pressure to show off your work in progress, sugar, half the magic is just being in a room with people who understand the creative slog. Best time tip: pack your own project (sketchbook, yarn, half-finished manuscript, whatever) since the gallery supplies the atmosphere and company, not your materials, and sit close enough to the screen to catch the good lines.
