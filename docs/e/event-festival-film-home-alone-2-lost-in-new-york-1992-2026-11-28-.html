Festival Film: Home Alone 2: Lost in New York (1992)

Saturday, November 28 · 4:30 PM

Philbrook Museum of Art

Philbrook's Festival Film series is screening Home Alone 2: Lost in New York, which means cozy chaos, Kevin McCallister outsmarting grown men, and that very specific holiday nostalgia all projected big and bright. This is about as low-pressure as a night out gets, darling, you watch a movie, you laugh, you leave. No mingling quota, no outfit stress, just a classic and a comfortable seat. Show up by 4:15 to grab a good seat before the room fills with everyone else craving that same nostalgia hit.
