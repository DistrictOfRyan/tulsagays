Zodiac Set with DJ Kylie

Friday, July 31 · 9:00 PM

Yellow Brick Road

Zodiac Set with DJ Kylie brings a low-pressure night of music and vibes to Yellow Brick Road, the kind of set where the dance floor and the bar stool both count as fully participating. Nobody's checking a guest list for plus-ones, honey, solo is a completely normal way to walk in here. It's the perfect night if you want queer energy in the room without needing to perform extrovert the whole time. Best time to have the best time: grab a seat at the bar around 9 when it opens, order something cold, and let DJ Kylie's set do the talking while you ease into the room at your own pace.
