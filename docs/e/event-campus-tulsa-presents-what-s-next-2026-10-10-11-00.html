Campus Tulsa Presents "What's Next?!"

Saturday, October 10 · 11:00 AM

Guthrie Green

What's Next is a free career and culture festival in Tulsa, built to connect college students with the people, employers and community that could shape their future. Over three days, student get real conversations with local employers, a taste of Tulsa's culture and a low-pressure look at building a career, and a life, in Tulsa could feel like. It's a Saturday thing, around 11:00 AM, over at Guthrie Green. Bring your whole personality. That's the only ticket this town really checks.
