Escándalo - Tulsa Pride Weekend Dance Competition

Friday, October 16 · 9:30 PM

Club Majestic

Escándalo turns Club Majestic into Tulsa Pride weekend's main dance competition, with doors at 9 and the real show kicking off at 9:30, so you've got a built-in window to get a drink and claim your spot before things heat up. This is the night to let the floor be the main event, dancers going for it under the lights while the rest of the room loses itself in the bass right alongside them. There's no wallflower tax here, sugar, half the fun is just being in a room where everybody's already decided to let loose. Get there right at doors so you can watch the floor fill up slow and pick your favorite corner before eleven o'clock, because that's when it gets shoulder to shoulder.
