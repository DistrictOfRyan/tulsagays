Once Upon A Drag

Tuesday, October 13 · 6:00 PM

Elote Cafe & Catering

Once Upon A Drag at Elote Cafe & Catering. Drag is Tulsa's most reliably spectacular live performance art, and this is your invitation to stop watching clips on your phone and actually be in the room where it's happening, which is a fundamentally different experience that no screen has ever fully managed to replicate. The lights, the music, the specific commitment of a performer who is giving you everything they have, it hits completely differently when you are standing there in front of it. Get close to the stage if you can. Bring singles because tipping your performers is not optional, it is the contract. Cheer loudly and mean it. Drag shows in Tulsa are run by real artists doing real work, and showing up and being present and putting money in their hands is how you support that work continuing.
