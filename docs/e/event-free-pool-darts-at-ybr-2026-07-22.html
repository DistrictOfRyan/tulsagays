Free Pool & Darts at YBR

Wednesday, July 22

Yellow Brick Road

Free Pool & Darts at Yellow Brick Road is the low-key hang where you don't need a plan, just a quarter's worth of nerve and a seat at the bar. The jukebox has taste, the lighting's got that soft neon glow, and nobody's watching the door to see who walks in alone. Post up near the pool table, buy the next round for whoever's terrible at darts (that's most of us, sugar), and let the night find its own rhythm. Best time to go: swing by early evening before the tables fill up, so you actually get a turn with the cue instead of just admiring it from the bar.
