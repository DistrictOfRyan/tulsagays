That 90s Party

Saturday, August 29

Cain's Ballroom

Cain's turns into a time machine for one night, TRL music, denim on denim, the whole nostalgia trip you didn't know you needed until the first bassline hits. Nobody's watching you, they're too busy singing along to something they forgot they knew every word to. Post up near the bar or a side wall early, that's your low-pressure perch, and let the dance floor build around you instead of the other way. By the second song you loved secretly in middle school, you'll have migrated six inches closer without even noticing, darling.
