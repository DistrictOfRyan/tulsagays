(Closed) OKEQ Health Clinic

Friday, June 5 · 1:00 PM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

Quick logistics note so your week runs smooth, this Friday 1 to 4 PM block of the OKEQ Health Clinic at the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center is marked closed. The clinic itself is a wonderfully affirming place for care, with appointments made at least 24 hours ahead, Blue Cross Blue Shield accepted, and a self-pay option for patients 18 and up. So if Friday afternoon was your plan, simply shift it to one of the open days earlier in the week. The care is worth the small reschedule. When you do book, aim for a morning slot on an open day so you are in and out with the day still ahead of you.
