DRAGNIFICENT! Drag Show

Thursday, November 5 · 10:00 PM

Club Majestic

DRAGNIFICENT! Turns Club Majestic into a late-night glitter storm with Shanel Sterling leading the charge, and honestly a drag show is one of the easiest nights out there is because your only job is to sit back, watch, and lose it when the wig comes off in one smooth motion. There's music, there's lip sync, there's a whole lot of heart under all that sequin, and tipping is basically a love language in that room. You don't need a group or a plan, drag shows are built for solo seats and strangers screaming together at the same fabulous moment. Best time to go: get there before the 10pm start so you can grab a seat near the stage, and bring small bills because tipping the queens is half the fun.
