Page, Pitch & Performance: The process of creating an original musical in amateur contexts

Wednesday, November 18 · 6:00 PM

This one's a peek behind the curtain of how an original musical actually gets built in the amateur theater world, straight from World Stage Theatre Company. You get the page, the pitch, and the performance all in one room, which is a rare backstage pass for anyone who's ever wondered how a show goes from an idea to a stage. It's the kind of night that rewards curiosity over small talk, so you can just watch and soak it in. Get there early enough to snag a seat with a clean sightline to the band, and tip generously when the tip jar comes around.
