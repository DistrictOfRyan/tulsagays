WTO/99

Monday, August 3 · 12:40 PM

Circle Cinema

WTO/99 is an immersive archival documentary that pulls you back into the streets of Seattle in 1999, when the World Trade Organization's big coming-out party collided head-on with a protest movement nobody saw coming. It's built from real footage of that clash, so you're not watching actors reenact history, you're watching it happen. Circle Cinema's afternoon matinee slot makes this an easy one to slide into a day off or a long lunch break. Grab the early 12:40 timing to beat the crowds and snag a seat with a clean view of the screen, sugar, Circle's smaller house means bad angles are avoidable if you're not last through the door.
