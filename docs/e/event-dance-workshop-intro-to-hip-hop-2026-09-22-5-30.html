Dance Workshop - Intro to Hip Hop

Tuesday, September 22 · 5:30 PM

NORTH HALL

North Hall 117 turns into a mirror-lined dance floor at 5:30 sharp, and honey, you don't need a single class on your resume to walk in. This is Intro to Hip Hop, built for the person who has never once heard "five six seven eight" and lived to talk about it. You'll pick up real moves, not just arm waves, and the room fills with the kind of nervous laughter that turns into real laughter fast. Wear shoes you can actually move in, sugar, because sneakers matter more than any dance experience ever will. Get there a few minutes before 5:30 and claim a spot near the mirror so you can actually see what your feet are doing.
