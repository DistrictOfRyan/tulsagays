Yellow Brick Road

Somewhere between a talent show and a group hug, sugar. Sign up the second you walk in, order the drink that unlocks your range, and remember the crowd roots hardest for whoever commits the most. It's a Thursday thing, over at Yellow Brick Road. Dress how it makes you feel, roll in a little early, and thank yourself later, sugar. Yellow Brick Road is Tulsa's only lesbian bar and one of the last in the US, and everyone is welcome. The gay-guy crowd tends to skip it, but it's an inclusive home for the whole community.
