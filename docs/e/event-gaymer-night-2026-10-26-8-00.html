Gaymer Night

Monday, October 26 · 8:00 PM

Tulsa Eagle

Gaymer Night at Tulsa Eagle is exactly what it sounds like, sugar: a room full of queer folks parked in front of screens, controllers in hand, zero pressure to make conversation unless you want to. You don't have to be a pro, you just have to show up and press buttons alongside people who'd rather talk strategy than small talk. The bar keeps the drinks flowing while the games keep your hands busy, which is basically an introvert's dream combo. Best time to go: slide in right at 8 when the first round is picking players, grab a seat near the controllers, and you'll be in a match before you've even finished your first drink.
