(Book Club) The Flyer Book Club

Tuesday, July 28 · 6:00 PM

Palomino Books and Music

Community newsroom. Digital publication. Nonprofit organization. Book club? That’s right, Flyers. We’re bringing Tulsans together to read, discuss and connect over books about Tulsa or by Tulsans. Whether you’re a seasoned reader or just getting back into books, everyone is welcome. Upcoming Book Club: Calling for a Blanket Dance (2022) is the debut novel by Mexican and Native American author Oscar Hokeah. The coming-of-age story explores cultural identity, intergenerational trauma and family resilience across Native and immigrant communities in the American Southwest. The story follows Ever Geimausaddle, a boy of Kiowa, Cherokee and Mexican descent, as he grows up navigating a web of competing cultures, economic hardships and family complexities. Need a copy? Mention the Flyer Book Club and get 10% off when purchasing a copy at Magic City Books or Palomino Books & Music. It's a Tuesday thing, around 6:00 PM, over at Palomino Books and Music. Put on the look, bring a friend or bring your nerve, and let Tulsa show you a good time.
