LOQAL LGBTQ+ Board Game Night, CDMX

Thursday, July 16 · 1:00 AM

El Ocho

LOQAL brings its board game night to El Ocho, another one built on language, culture, and pride, except this time your icebreaker is a game board instead of small talk. Tables fill up fast with everything from strategy games to the silly ones that get the whole room laughing, and nobody's going to side-eye you for asking to learn the rules. It's a genuinely easy way to sit at a table with new people without the pressure of a dance floor or a mic. Get there early to snag a spot at a table that's already picking a game you actually know, and don't be shy about asking to join a group mid-round, that's exactly how this works.
