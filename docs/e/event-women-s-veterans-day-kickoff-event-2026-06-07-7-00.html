Women's Veterans Day Kickoff Event

Sunday, June 7 · 7:00 PM

Coffee Bunker

The Women's Veterans Day Kickoff Event at Coffee Bunker opens a month dedicated to honoring, connecting, and empowering women veterans in the community. This veteran-led gathering is built to be a genuinely welcoming space, the kind of room where shared experience does the introductions for you. You might have served, you might support someone who did, or you might simply want to show up in solidarity, and you will find warmth here either way. There is real strength in a community that takes care of its own. Arrive around the 7 PM start, and if a full room feels like a lot, set the easy goal of connecting with just one or two people, that is plenty for a first time.
