Sunday Showdown Open Talent Night

Sunday, November 29

Club Majestic

Sunday Showdown Open Talent Night hits Club Majestic with doors at 9pm and the show kicking off at 11pm, and darling, this is a room built for watching people be brave so you don't have to be. Order something with a little sparkle to it, find your spot at the bar, and just let the night unfold around you while strangers take the stage and go for it. It's late, it's loud, and it's exactly the kind of low-stakes chaos that makes a Sunday night feel like it mattered. Say hi to whoever's beside you at the bar like you've known them for years, that one line does more work than you'd think, and hold onto your spot near the stage once the talent starts because that's where the energy lives.
