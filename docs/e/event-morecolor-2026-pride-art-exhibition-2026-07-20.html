MOREcolor 2026: Pride Art Exhibition

Monday, July 20

MOREcolor 2026 fills the walls with fine art from 2SLGBTQ+ artists all in celebration of Pride season, and every piece is there for you to actually look at up close, no velvet rope required. This is the kind of outing that asks nothing of you but your eyes and your attention, perfect for a night when the couch feels safer than a crowd. The work is also for sale, so if something stops you in your tracks, sugar, you can take it home instead of just remembering it. Best time to go: give yourself a slow lap around the whole room before you decide on a favorite, the best pieces rarely grab you first.
