MOREcolor 2026: Pride Art Exhibition

Monday, July 20

Allie Jensen Memorial Art Gallery (Equality Center)

MOREcolor 2026 fills the walls with queer artists working at the top of their game, paint practically still wet on some of these pieces. This isn't a tiptoe-through-a-museum kind of gallery, darling, it's loud color and personal stories hung salon-style for you to get close to. Take your time with each piece, the artist behind it is probably somewhere in the room if you want to say so. Swing by today while the show's up, sugar, and budget extra time near the entrance wall, that's where the showstoppers tend to hang.
