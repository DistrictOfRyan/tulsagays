My Brother's Killer- Documentary Screening and Discussion

Sunday, October 11 · 12:15 PM

Circle Cinema

My Brother's Killer is a true crime documentary screening at Circle Cinema about a hate crime case in Los Angeles that got cracked wide open thanks to tips and work that came out of right here in Tulsa, sugar. It's the kind of story that makes you sit up straighter, knowing your own city played a part, and it's followed by a live discussion, so you get context and conversation, not just credits rolling into silence. Documentaries like this hit different in a theater, surrounded by other people feeling the same gut punch moments you are. Best time to go: stick around after the lights come up for the discussion, that's where the real texture comes out that the film alone doesn't have time for.
