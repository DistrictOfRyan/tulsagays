The Sunday Showdown! Open Talent Night

Sunday, August 2 · 9:00 PM

Club Majestic

The Sunday Showdown is Club Majestic's open talent night, doors swing open at 9 but the real show doesn't ignite until 11 when Shanel Sterling takes the mic and starts building Tulsa's next star right in front of you. If you're there to perform, scream for a friend, or just soak up the chaos from a barstool, this is the kind of night that turns strangers into your people by last call. Get there before 11, baby, grab a spot with a clear sightline to the stage, because once Shanel starts working the crowd you will not want to be squeezed in the back trying to see over shoulders.
