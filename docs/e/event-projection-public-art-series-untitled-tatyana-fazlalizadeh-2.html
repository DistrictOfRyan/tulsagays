PROJECTION PUBLIC ART SERIES: 'UNTITLED' TATYANA FAZLALIZADEH

Monday, June 1 · 10:00 AM

Flagship / Tulsa Artist Fellowship

PROJECTION is a new rotating public art series conceived and directed by artist Tatyana Fazlalizadeh, cast onto the exterior wall of the Tulsa Artist Fellowship's studio building downtown. It is public art in the truest sense, free, out in the open, and there for anyone who happens to look up. For introverts, this is the dream outing, all the beauty of a gallery with none of the pressure to mingle or make conversation. Just you, the work, and the city around it. Wander by on your own schedule and start from the far edge of the wall, taking it in slowly before the foot traffic picks up.
