Fall Break at Fallbrook

Wednesday, October 14 · 10:00 AM

Philbrook Museum of Art

Fall Break at Fallbrook is Philbrook Museum's low-key answer to a packed holiday week, come dressed exactly as you are, wander the galleries and the gardens at your own pace, and leave the second you've had enough. There's no performance here, no pressure to mingle past your comfort, just beautiful art and grounds doing the heavy lifting while you float through. It's the gentlest possible way to get out of the house on a day off, perfect if you want company without needing much conversation. Best time to go is right at opening, before the crowds build, so the gardens are quiet and you can take the long way to every room.
