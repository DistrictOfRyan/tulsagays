Spotlight Student Art Exhibition | McFarlin Library

Monday, October 5

McFarlin Library

The Spotlight Student Art Exhibition at McFarlin Library is the second in a rotating series supported by The Hardesty Family Foundation, showcasing student work in a space built just for it. This is a low-key, no-pressure way to spend an afternoon, wander the stacks and let the art stop you in your tracks. It's free, it's quiet, and it's the kind of outing that counts as getting out of the house without requiring a drop of small talk. Swing by on a weekday afternoon when the library's slow, that's when you get the gallery practically to yourself.
