Queer Crafters Club

Thursday, June 4 · 6:00 PM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

Queer Crafters Club is a bring-your-own-project hang in the Gallery at the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center, running 6 to 8 PM on Thursdays. Knitting, sketching, beading, gluing, whatever your medium, you bring it and work on it surrounded by people doing the same. The beauty of a craft night is that the project gives your hands something to do and the conversation just happens around it, no pressure to fill silences. It is one of the gentlest ways to meet people, especially if small talk usually drains you. Arrive right at 6, claim a spot at the table, and let the making do the social heavy lifting for you.
