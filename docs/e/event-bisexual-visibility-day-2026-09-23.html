Bisexual Visibility Day

Wednesday, September 23

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

Bisexual Visibility Day at the Equality Center is a whole afternoon built around not having to justify a thing about who you are. Come as you are, mingle at your own pace, and let the room hold space for you instead of the other way around. This one isn't about performing pride, it's about just existing in it. Best time to have it: arrive early, say hello to one person before you find your spot, and let the conversation build from there.
