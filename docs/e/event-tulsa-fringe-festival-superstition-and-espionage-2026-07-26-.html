Tulsa Fringe Festival: Superstition and Espionage

Sunday, July 26 · 12:30 PM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

Superstition and Espionage is a Fringe Fest show built for the solo mission, honey, the kind of afternoon where you don't need a plus one to have a good time. Claim a corner, settle into your seat, and let the story do all the heavy lifting while you just sit back and enjoy it. Going alone here isn't a consolation prize, it's the smart move, since you get the whole show without splitting your attention. Arrive by 12:15 to grab a seat in that back corner with the clearest sightline, and let the lights go down before you even think about small talk.
