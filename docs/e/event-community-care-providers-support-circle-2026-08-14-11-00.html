Community Care Providers Support Circle

Friday, August 14 · 11:00 AM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

The Community Care Providers Support Circle meets late morning at the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center for anyone doing the emotionally heavy work of caregiving in this community, nurses, social workers, chosen-family caretakers, all of it. It's a circle, not a stage, so you can listen for the first twenty minutes and only speak when something in you finally wants to. Baby, the whole point is that nobody here is going to make you perform okay-ness, you get to just be tired in good company. Best time tip: arrive at 11 sharp so you snag a chair on the edge of the circle, and bring your coffee, this one runs on quiet solidarity, not small talk.
