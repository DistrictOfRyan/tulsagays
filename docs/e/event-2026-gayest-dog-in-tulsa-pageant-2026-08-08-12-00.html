2026 Gayest Dog in Tulsa Pageant

Saturday, August 8 · 12:00 PM

Tulsa Little Theatre

Twisted Arts brings the Gayest Dog in Tulsa Pageant to Tulsa Little Theatre, and darling, it's exactly what it sounds like: proud pups strutting for a crowd that lives for a good fundraiser with a wink. This is a daytime, low-pressure kind of fabulous, the kind where you can clap for a chihuahua in a tutu and call it self-care. Every ticket and tail wag goes toward something good, so your applause is doing double duty. Get there right at noon when the doors open so you snag a seat with a clear sightline to the runway, because the front rows fill up fast once the leashes start parading.
