Tulsa Leather Community (TLC) Oklahoma

Wednesday, October 7 · 6:30 PM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

Tulsa Leather Community (TLC) Oklahoma meets at the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center at 6:30, and it's exactly the kind of room where you can ease in slow. Nobody's going to rush you into anything, this crowd knows that watching and learning is its own kind of belonging. It's a chance to meet people steeped in leather culture and history without any pressure to dive in headfirst. Show up right at 6:30 and post up near the back wall at first, that's your best vantage point to read the room before you decide how close you want to get.
