Tulsa Leather Community (TLC) Oklahoma

Wednesday, August 5 · 6:30 PM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

Tulsa Leather Community (TLC) Oklahoma gathers at the Equality Center for their regular meetup, and it's built for exactly the kind of night where you don't have to work the room to belong in it. Nobody's grabbing your arm and dragging you into the middle of things, you can hang near the doorway, get a feel for the group, and ease in at your own speed. Leather community spaces have a reputation for being intense, but a lot of it is just people who like structure, respect, and a clear sense of where they stand, which is honestly restful once you settle in. Best time to go: arrive close to 6:30 while things are still settling and introductions are happening in smaller clusters, easier to catch than wading into a packed room later.
