Prop Swappers of Tulsa Monthly meet up @ GUTHRIE GREEN

Sunday, July 12 · 1:00 PM

Guthrie Green

An event for plant enthusiast to meet new people, learn, and swap propagations on the patio Let's spread some plant love this month! Join us for our monthly Plant Propagation Swap at the GUTHRIE GREEN patio in downtown Tulsa. If its too hot we will be moving the event to the FLY LOFT right across the street. Bring your plant babies and trade them for some new green companions, meet new people and enjoy something from the cafe. 🌱🪴 Remember, bringing propagations arent required to attend the event! This event is organized and hosted by EBONY JONES , if you are interested in being a vendor or participant please reach out to HER !! So, get propagating! #plantpropagation, #plantswap, or #plantlover It's a Sunday thing, around 1:00 PM, over at Guthrie Green. Dress how it makes you feel, roll in a little early, and thank yourself later, sugar.
