Raise Your Spiritual IQ! Spiritual Discussion- Reinvent Yourself Spiritually

Sunday, June 7 · 7:00 PM

Martin Regional Library

Raise Your Spiritual IQ! is a discussion-based gathering at Martin Regional Library digging into the bigger questions, this round on moving past the past and breaking free from karma and reincarnation. It is exploratory and open-minded rather than dogmatic, a space to think out loud and listen to others do the same. For the contemplative type, this is an easy, low-pressure outing, you can absorb the conversation and chime in only when something genuinely moves you. No belief required at the door, just curiosity. Arrive a few minutes before 7 so you can settle into a seat and let the discussion unfold at its own pace.
