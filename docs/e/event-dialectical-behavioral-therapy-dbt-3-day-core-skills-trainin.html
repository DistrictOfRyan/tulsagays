Dialectical Behavioral Therapy (DBT) 3-Day Core Skills Training

Thursday, June 4 · 9:00 AM

The DBT 3-Day Core Skills Training is a hands-on chance to learn dialectical behavioral therapy fundamentals, the real, usable tools for managing intense emotions, distress, and relationships. Running over three days from 9 AM, it goes deeper than a quick workshop, giving the skills room to actually sink in. For anyone who has felt at the mercy of their own emotional weather, this is the kind of practical, grounding knowledge that sticks with you. The people drawn to a room like this tend to be thoughtful and kind. Arrive in time for the 9 AM start so you catch the foundation, since each day builds on the last.
