SLASH AND SLAY : A MURDER MYSTERY DRAG SHOW

Sunday, October 25 · 6:00 PM

Tulsa Eagle

Slash and Slay is a full drag murder mystery, so you're not just watching a show, you're solving one alongside a cast of queens who are clearly having the time of their lives being suspects. Expect twists, big reveals, and the kind of audience participation that makes strangers into co-conspirators by intermission. It's part whodunit, part drag show, and completely its own thing. Best time to go: get to 1338 E 3rd by 6 sharp to grab a seat with a clear view of the stage, you don't want to miss a single clue or a single wig reveal.
