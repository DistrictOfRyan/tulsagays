Sunday Showdown Open Talent Night

Sunday, June 7 · 9:00 PM

Club Majestic

Sunday Showdown Open Talent Night at Club Majestic is where the city's LGBTQIA+ talent comes to show out, with doors at 9 and the show kicking off at 11. Performers bring it, the room gets loud, and the whole thing runs on the kind of community energy that only happens when people show up for each other. Order a drink, plant yourself at the bar, and strike up a conversation with whoever lands next to you, that is half the fun. Sunday nightlife in Tulsa stays strong because folks keep walking through the door. Get there around doors at 9 to ease into the room before it fills, and let the night build from there.
