Sunday Showdown Open Talent Night

Sunday, November 22

Club Majestic

Honey, Sunday Showdown Open Talent Night at Club Majestic is the kind of chaos that only works because everybody in that room is rooting for whoever's brave enough to grab the mic. Doors open at 9 PM and the show doesn't kick off till 11, so there's a whole stretch of time built in just for you to ease into the room, grab a drink, and get comfortable before anything even starts. Open talent means you never know what you're walking into, lip sync, stand up, a surprise vocal number, and that unpredictability is half the fun of the night. Best time tip: get there right at doors and claim a spot near the bar early, that stretch before showtime is when the room is loosest and easiest to strike up a conversation, order something with a little sparkle, and let the talent lineup do the rest.
