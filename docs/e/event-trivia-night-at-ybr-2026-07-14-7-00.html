Trivia Night at YBR

Tuesday, July 14 · 7:00 PM

Yellow Brick Road

Yellow Brick Road turns into a low-stakes battlefield every trivia night, the host cracking jokes over the mic while teams huddle over half-remembered movie facts and one too many bar napkins for notes. Round up a couple strangers at the bar, sugar, name your team something that'll make the host blush when he reads it out loud, and play like there's rent riding on the answer. You don't need a squad walking in, you'll have one by round two. Best time to have the best time: arrive by 6:45 to snag a table near the speakers before the room fills, and sign up early, teams lock in fast.
