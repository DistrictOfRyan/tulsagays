The Sunday Showdown! Open Talent Night

Sunday, August 9

Club Majestic

The Sunday Showdown is Club Majestic's open talent showcase, the spot where you catch up-and-coming performers before everybody else knows their names, hosted by Shanel Sterling and friends who know exactly how to hype a newcomer. It's rawer and looser than a polished weekly show, which is half the fun, you're watching people take real swings. Doors open at 9pm but the show itself doesn't kick off until 11, so there's a solid window to grab a drink and stake your spot. Get there close to doors if you want a seat near the stage, sugar, that two-hour gap between doors and showtime is exactly when the room fills in.
