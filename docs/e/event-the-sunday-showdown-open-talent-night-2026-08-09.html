The Sunday Showdown! Open Talent Night

Sunday, August 9

Club Majestic

The Sunday Showdown is Club Majestic's open talent night, the stage where brand new performers step up and try out their acts in front of a real crowd. Doors open at 9 PM with the show kicking off at 11 PM, so you've got time to grab a drink and get settled before the talent starts flowing. This is the kind of night where you might catch someone's very first performance, the one they'll be talking about years from now as where it all started. Get there at doors and claim a spot with a clear sightline, open talent nights move fast between acts and you don't want to miss the surprise breakout of the night.
