Twisted Arts Film Fest: Downtown Screening

Thursday, September 24 · 7:00 PM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

The Downtown Screening for Twisted Arts Film Fest is exactly the kind of night out an introvert can love: dim lights, a shared screen, and zero obligation to talk to anyone until the credits roll. You get real Tulsa filmmaking talent without a single ounce of small talk pressure. It's the easiest kind of communal experience there is, sit down, watch something good, feel connected anyway. Best time to have it: get there by 6:45 to grab a seat before the lights go down, then stick around after for the chatter in the lobby if you're feeling brave.
