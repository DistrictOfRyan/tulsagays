Muskogee Pride

Saturday, September 19 · 11:00 AM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

Muskogee Pride rolls into Dennis R. Neill Equality Center at 11am with a whole day built for people who'd rather stand at the edge of the crowd before they dive in, so darling, that includes you. Nobody's handing you a name tag and shoving you into a conga line, you get to take in the booths, the music, the whole parade of color at your own pace. That slow-build energy is exactly what makes it worth prying yourself off the couch, because the moment you're ready to join in, everybody there already expects you to. Best time to arrive is right at 11am when the crowd is still thin, sugar, so you can scope the whole layout before it fills in.
