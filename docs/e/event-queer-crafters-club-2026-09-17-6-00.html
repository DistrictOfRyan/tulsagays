Queer Crafters Club

Thursday, September 17 · 6:00 PM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

Queer Crafters Club is exactly what it sounds like, sugar: a room full of your people, a pile of supplies, and zero expectation that you know what a running stitch is. Show up at Dennis R. Neill Equality Center, 621 E 4th St, with just your two hands, because everything else, from felt to embroidery floss, is already laid out on the table. It's the kind of low-stakes hang where the conversation flows easier than the craft technique, and nobody's grading your glue gun skills. Best time to walk in is right at 6pm sharp, honey, so you snag a seat at the main table instead of the folding chair way in the back corner.
