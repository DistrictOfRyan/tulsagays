Queer Crafters Club

Thursday, August 6 · 6:00 PM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

Queer Crafters Club sets up shop at the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center, 621 E 4th St, with tables full of supplies and zero expectation that you know what a stitch even is. It's the rare queer hangout where your hands stay busy and your brain gets to wander, which makes for dangerously easy small talk with strangers who are also just trying to finish a coaster. Bring a project if you've got one, or show up empty-handed, baby, there's always something to pick up. Grab a seat near the middle of the table right at 6, that's where the passing-supplies chatter starts and the friendships sneak up on you.
