Trivia Night at YBR

Tuesday, July 21 · 7:00 PM

Yellow Brick Road

Trivia Night at Yellow Brick Road turns a room full of strangers into a scrappy little league of chosen family, one smart-aleck answer at a time. Round up whoever's nearby, slap a name on your team that'll make the host blush, and play like there's rent riding on round three. Nobody's grading your gay trivia knowledge at the door, honey, so wander in solo and you'll have a team before the first question's read. Best time to go: get there before 7 so you can claim a table near the host stand, where the mic banter is loudest and the drink orders move fastest.
