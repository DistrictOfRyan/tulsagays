Solaris with Tulsa Global Alliance

Tuesday, August 18 · 7:00 PM

Circle Cinema

Circle Cinema is screening Solaris, presented with Tulsa Global Alliance in partnership with Zelenograd, Tulsa's Russian Sister City, so this isn't just a movie night, it's a genuine bit of cultural exchange landing right here in town. Solaris is slow and haunting and gorgeous, the kind of film that rewards a dark theater and total attention rather than a phone in your lap on the couch. Best time to have the best time: grab tickets ahead of time since they're already on sale, darling, and get there early enough to settle in before the lights go down, because this one asks you to be fully present from frame one.
