Exploring Watercolor: Sketching Sweets

Thursday, July 23

Living Arts of Tulsa

Enjoy a feast for the eyes as we sketch sweets together! This 2 hour workshop with Sara Anais will guide you through sketching the food in front of you in a quick, loose, and expressive style, helping you overcome the fear of mistakes. Learn how to use watercolor and ink to simplify detailed areas and work your way through a sketch. This workshop is open to any skill level, and all supplies will be included. Of course, treats will be eaten after sketching! It's a Thursday thing, over at Living Arts of Tulsa. Dress how it makes you feel, roll in a little early, and thank yourself later, sugar.
