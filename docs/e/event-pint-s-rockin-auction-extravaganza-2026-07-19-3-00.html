PINT's Rockin' Auction Extravaganza

Sunday, July 19 · 3:00 PM

Venue Shrine

Paws In Need Tulsa throws its Rockin' Auction Extravaganza at Venue Shrine, and it is the rare fundraiser that actually slaps. Local rockers Cobi Lalas open with the rock classics from the 70s, 80s, and 90s you will absolutely sing along to, then swing into their own quantum groove rock, all grunge grit and red-dirt soul. While the music plays you can wander a silent auction stacked with artwork, collectibles, experiences, and musical instruments, and every ticket and every winning bid funds PINT's transport program that drives homeless animals to rescue partners across the country. It runs 3 to 7pm, all heart, all good cause. Tickets are on StubWire. Best-time tip for the shy ones: come right at 3 while the auction tables are fresh and the room is still easy to work, scope your bids early, then settle in for the second set when the band gets weird and wonderful.
