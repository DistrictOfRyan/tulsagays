Live Music Night at Sisserous

Thursday, August 13

Tulsa

Live Music Night at Sisserous means real musicians plugging in and filling the room with sound, sugar, the kind of night where you can just exist in a corner and let the music do the socializing for you. It's low stakes as far as nights out go, no need to make conversation, just sip something good and let the set wash over you. The energy builds as the night goes, so even if you slip in quiet you won't stay that way for long. Grab that back corner table early, order your drink, and let the first few songs pull you closer to the action once you're ready.
