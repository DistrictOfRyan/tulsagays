Tulsa Shakespeare Co. presents Hamlet

Saturday, October 31 · 7:30 PM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

Tulsa Shakespeare Co. Is bringing the whole bloody, brooding mess of Hamlet to the Equality Center on Halloween night, and darling, that is a mood match made in heaven. Nobody's clocking whether you rolled up solo, the lights go down and it's just you and Denmark falling apart in the best way. It's live theater on a night most people spend drunk in a costume they regret, so you'll actually remember this one. Best time to go: slip in a few minutes early, grab an aisle seat, and let the first ghost scene do the work of pulling you out of your own head.
