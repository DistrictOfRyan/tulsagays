Silent Book Club at Rivendell Books and Baubles

Thursday, July 16 · 6:00 PM

Rivendell Books and Baubles

Join us for our Monthly Silent Book Club at Rivendell Books and Baubles! Every third Thursday | 6:00 PM - 8:00 PM Step into our cozy bookstore for an evening of literary discussion, peaceful reading, and bookish camaraderie. Whether you're a casual reader or a devoted bibliophile, you'll find your people here! 📚 Event Format: 6:00 - 6:30 PM: Social Hour • Arrive and get settled • Grab drinks and snacks from our coffee bar • Share what you're currently reading • Connect with fellow book lovers 6:30 - 7:30 PM: Quiet Reading Hour • Dedicated peaceful reading time • Bring your current read or browse our shelves • Enjoy the tranquil atmosphere among fellow readers 7:30 - 8:00 PM: Optional Social Time • Discuss what you read • Exchange book recommendations • Connect with your reading community • Wind down the evening at your own pace What to expect: • A welcoming community of fellow readers • Comfortable seating for reading • Light refreshments available at our coffee bar • A peaceful, distraction-free environment • The magical ambiance of being surrounded by books Bring whatever book you're currently reading - this isn't a traditional book club where everyone reads the same book. Instead, it's a dedicated time to read, share recommendations, and connect with other book lovers! Mark your calendar for the third Thursday of every month and join our growing community of readers! Location: Rivendell Books and Baubles 2034 W Houston St; Broken Arrow, OK 74012 Space is limited - we recommend arriving a few minutes early to get settled in. Questions? Feel free to reach out to us! It's a Thursday thing, around 6:00 PM, over at Rivendell Books and Baubles. Put on the look, bring a friend or bring your nerve, and let Tulsa show you a good time.
