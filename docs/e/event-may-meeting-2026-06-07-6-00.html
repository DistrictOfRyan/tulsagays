May Meeting

Sunday, June 7 · 6:00 PM

Poppi’s Spa + Lounge

The May Meeting gathers the group at Poppi's Spa + Lounge for an evening of genuine rejuvenation, sauna, salt room, and color therapy pod all on the menu. Lounge passes run $40 for two hours and can be reserved online or in person, with upgrades available if you want to go deeper. It is a rare social outing that actually leaves you more relaxed than when you arrived, and a lovely way to support a locally owned business at the same time. Self-care and good company, together. Reserve your pass ahead of the 6 PM start so your spot is locked in, then arrive a few minutes early to ease into the calm.
