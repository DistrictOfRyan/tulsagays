Babes & Bi-cons Dance Party at YBR

Saturday, August 1 · 9:30 PM

Yellow Brick Road

Babes & Bi-cons Dance Party at YBR at Yellow Brick Road, 2630 E 15th St. Quick PSA, because too many people get this wrong: Yellow Brick Road is Tulsa's only lesbian bar and one of the last left in the country, and it is absolutely for everybody. The gay guys skip it and they shouldn't, the welcome mat is out for the whole community. Pull up to the cave. Gay bars are the physical proof that we exist and that we deserve a room that belongs to us without asterisks, and YBR holds that down for the girls and for anyone who walks in with respect. The bartenders know the regulars, the music is better than you expect, and the night is better with more of us in it. Show up.
