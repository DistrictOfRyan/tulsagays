DRAGNIFICENT! Drag Show

Thursday, August 20 · 10:00 PM

Club Majestic

DRAGNIFICENT! Is Club Majestic's weekly Thursday drag show, hosted by the one and only Shanel Sterling, who runs a tight, high-energy show every single week. This isn't a rare treat you have to plan around, darling, it's a standing Thursday date with big numbers, bigger wigs, and a host who knows how to work a room. Best time to have the best time: get there before 10 so you can grab a seat with a sightline to the stage, because once Shanel steps out the crowd surges forward and standing in the back is no way to see rhinestones properly. Bring a few singles for tips, honey, the girls remember who shows love.
