HotMess Kickball Fall 2026 Free Open Play

Wednesday, September 23 · 6:30 PM

McClure Park Softball Field

HotMess Sports Tulsa is throwing open kickball at McClure Park, and every skill level is genuinely welcome, from "I have a glove I've never used" to "I was a ringer in middle school." This is rec league energy at its finest: casual, silly, and way more about the group chat afterward than the score. Bring a lawn chair if you're spectating, or just wing it if you're playing. Best time to have it: show up right at 6:30 when teams are forming, plant yourself near the dugout, and you'll be drafted onto a team before you finish tying your shoes.
