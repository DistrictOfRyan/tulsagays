Midweek Market at Santa Fe Square!

Wednesday, July 15 · 4:30 PM

Midweek Market at Santa Fe Square! - Tulsa Arts District

Midweek Market at Santa Fe Square turns a Wednesday evening in the Tulsa Arts District into a stroll past local vendors, food trucks, and live music with zero pressure to perform extrovert energy. This market runs on people who'd rather browse first and chat when it feels right, so you can circle the same jewelry table twice without anyone thinking twice about it. It's the kind of low-stakes evening that eases you into being out among people, sugar, not the kind that throws you into the deep end. Best time to have the best time: arrive right at 4:30 while the light's still golden and the crowd's thin, then let the smell of whatever's on the grill lead you to your first stop.
