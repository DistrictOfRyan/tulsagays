Figments of My Imagination by Rickey Mizell, Annual Halloween Art Show and Sale at Chrysalis Salon & Spa

Wednesday, October 21 · 12:00 PM

Chrysalis Salon & Spa

Figments of My Imagination brings Rickey Mizell's Halloween themed art straight into Chrysalis Salon & Spa, which means you get to browse a whole gallery's worth of eerie, playful pieces in a space that smells like good shampoo instead of stuffy gallery air. It's a show and a sale, so every piece on the wall is something you can actually walk out the door with if it catches you right. This is the rare art event where nobody's watching you too closely, so take your time, lean in close, read the little cards, let something weird and wonderful win you over. Wander through slowly and ask about the pieces that catch your eye first, sugar, because the ones that grab you fastest are usually the ones worth taking home.
