Figments of My Imagination by Rickey Mizell - Annual Halloween Art Show and Sale at Chrysalis Salon & Spa

Wednesday, October 21 · 12:00 PM

Chrysalis Salon & Spa

Rickey Mizell's Figments of My Imagination turns Chrysalis Salon & Spa into a cozy little Halloween art gallery, part spooky season, part pampering, zero chaos. You can wander the pieces, sip a little something, and shop original art without ever feeling like you have to perform extrovert for the room. It's the rare Halloween event where mellow is the whole point. Pop in early around noon when the salon chairs are open and you can actually sit a spell while you take it all in.
