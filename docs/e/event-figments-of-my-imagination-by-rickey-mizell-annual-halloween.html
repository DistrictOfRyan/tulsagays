Figments of My Imagination by Rickey Mizell - Annual Halloween Art Show and Sale at Chrysalis Salon & Spa

Wednesday, October 21 · 12:00 PM

Chrysalis Salon & Spa

Rickey Mizell's Figments of My Imagination takes over Chrysalis Salon & Spa for an annual Halloween art show and sale that's as relaxed as it is eerie. You get to browse spooky, imaginative work in a salon setting with a drink in hand, no gallery stiffness required. It's a laid back way to do Halloween season without committing to a costume or a crowd. Wander in early afternoon when the salon's got breathing room, take your time with each piece, and if something catches your eye, ask about it, the sale part means it could come home with you.
