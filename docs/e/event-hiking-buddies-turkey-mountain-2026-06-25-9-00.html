Hiking Buddies (Turkey Mountain)

Thursday, June 25 · 9:00 AM

Turkey Mountain

The cedar canopy at Turkey Mountain does this thing in morning light where everything smells like summer and looks like it was arranged just for you, and Thursday's Hiking Buddies group sets off at 9 AM into exactly that, honey. This is a 3-5 mile beginner and intermediate friendly route, which means it's genuinely doable and not a trick, and the group that shows up is the kind of people who walk and talk and make the miles feel like nothing. Fresh air, moving legs, and the quiet satisfaction of finishing a trail before lunch: that is a whole mood, darling. Show up a few minutes early at the trailhead, wear real hiking shoes (the rocks don't care about your aesthetic, baby), and bring more water than you think you need.
