Comedy Drag Karaoke

Wednesday, September 9 · 9:00 PM

Lazaro Cardenas 341

Comedy Drag Karaoke at Lazaro Cardenas 341 is part talent show, part group hug, all glorious chaos, where regular people grab the mic between drag numbers and the room cheers everybody on. It's the kind of night built for belting out your one karaoke song while queens work the crowd around you. The energy only builds as the sign-up sheet fills in, so getting there early means a better slot and a longer runway to enjoy the show. Best time to have the best time: walk in right at 9, sign up immediately before the list gets long, and order the drink that gets you unlocked enough to go up there.
