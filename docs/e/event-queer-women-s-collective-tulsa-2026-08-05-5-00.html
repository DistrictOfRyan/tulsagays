Queer Women's Collective Tulsa

Wednesday, August 5 · 5:00 PM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

Queer Women's Collective Tulsa gathers at the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center for the gentlest kind of hangout, no performance required, no dress code, no pressure to stay past the point you're comfortable. It's built for the exact energy you've got today, chatty or quiet, an hour or the whole evening. Bring whatever you're nursing, a coffee, a rough week, a new haircut you want someone to notice, and let the conversation find you. Slide in right at 5 while the room's still small, sugar, that's when the best one-on-one conversations happen before the crowd fills in.
