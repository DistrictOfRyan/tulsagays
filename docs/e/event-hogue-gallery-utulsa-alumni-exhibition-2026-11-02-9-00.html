Hogue Gallery | UTulsa Alumni Exhibition

Monday, November 2 · 9:00 AM

Alexandre Hogue Gallery

The Alexandre Hogue Gallery is hosting its UTulsa Alumni Exhibition, a collection of work from folks who came up through the university's art program, which means you're getting a whole range of styles and stories under one roof. It's quiet, it's beautiful, and there's not a single ounce of small talk required unless you want it, baby. This is the kind of outing that counts as "getting out of the house" while still letting you move at your own pace and breathe. Best time to go: morning, right after the 9 AM start, when the gallery's hushed and you've got the art practically to yourself.
