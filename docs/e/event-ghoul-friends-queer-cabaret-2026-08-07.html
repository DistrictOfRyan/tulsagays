Ghoul Friends Queer Cabaret

Friday, August 7

LATCH FEST throws open its curtain tonight with Ghoul Friends Queer Cabaret, the kind of spooky-glam variety show where the lip syncs land somewhere between camp and chills, backed by DJ Katnip keeping the floor thumping between numbers. This is the launch party, baby, the first domino in a whole festival, and the energy is freshest before the crowd's even found its legs. Wear something you can dance in, because Katnip doesn't believe in dead air between sets. Get there for the opening number, don't slide in during intermission thinking you'll catch the vibe cold, sugar, the vibe IS the opening number.
