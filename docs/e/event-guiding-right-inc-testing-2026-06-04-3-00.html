Guiding Right Inc. Testing

Thursday, June 4 · 3:00 PM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

Guiding Right Inc. brings free HIV and Hepatitis-C testing to the OKEQ Health Clinic at the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center on the first and third Thursday of each month, 3 to 6 PM. Knowing your status is one of the most caring things you can do for yourself and the people you love, and this crew makes the whole thing quick, private, and completely judgment-free. There is no cost and no lecture, just real prevention resources and people who treat you with respect. It is far less of a big deal than the anxiety leading up to it. Drop in anytime between 3 and 6, no appointment required, and aim for earlier in the window if you would rather skip any wait.
