Pandora's Box

Friday, October 23 · 9:30 PM

Club Majestic

Pandora's Box doesn't care if you dance, mingle, or just hold up a wall with a drink in hand, because the whole point is a room full of people who are simply happier with you in it, sugar. Doors open at 9 with the show running 9:30 to 10:30, so there's a built in window to get settled before anything really kicks off. This is the low pressure kind of night out that proves you can show up solo and still have a genuinely good time without performing for anyone. Get there right at doors so you can claim a comfortable spot before the room fills in, and let the show come to you instead of chasing it.
