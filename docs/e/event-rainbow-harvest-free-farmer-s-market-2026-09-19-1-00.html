Rainbow Harvest: Free Farmer's Market

Saturday, September 19 · 1:00 PM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

Rainbow Harvest's Free Farmer's Market sets up at Dennis R. Neill Equality Center at 1pm with local vendors and artists spreading out their goods, and honey, resisting all of it is not really on the table. It's a market, not a mixer, so you can wander at your own speed, no forced small talk required unless you want it, though half the fun is chatting with the person who made whatever you're eyeing. Bring cash, because plenty of these artists don't run cards, and bring a tote bag, because you will be carrying things home. Best time to go is right at 1pm when the booths are freshest and the good stuff hasn't sold out yet, sugar.
