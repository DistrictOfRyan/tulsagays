Allies Zine Training Series

Friday, October 16 · 6:00 PM

Oklahoma City / Tulsa area

This Allies Zine Training Series teaches you how to put together quick, shareable zines that pack real know-your-rights info and community safety steps into something easy to hand off. With ICE's local leadership shifting right now, having clear, portable info ready to pass around matters more than ever, darling. You'll walk out with actual skills, not just good intentions, plus a finished zine you can photocopy and spread around town. Bring a notebook and a friend or two, because the best zines get made when you're bouncing ideas off someone sitting right next to you.
