Sunday Showdown Open Talent Night

Sunday, November 1

Club Majestic

Sunday Showdown Open Talent Night at Club Majestic is an open floor for whoever's brave enough to grab the mic, doors at 9, show hitting its stride by 11, so you've got a whole window to ease in. Order yourself something with a little sparkle, plant yourself near the bar, and strike up a conversation with whoever's standing next to you like you've known them for years, sugar, that's half the fun of a night like this. You never know if you're about to watch a lip sync for the ages or a brand new performer find their legs for the first time, and that unpredictability is the whole draw. Best time to arrive is right at doors, 9 PM, so you can snag a good spot near the stage before the 11 PM show fills the room shoulder to shoulder.
