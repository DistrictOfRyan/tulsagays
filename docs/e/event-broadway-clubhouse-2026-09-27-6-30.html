Tulsa Gays · LGBTQ+ Event

Broadway Clubhouse

Sunday, September 27 · 6:30 PM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

Every month, the Lynn Riggs Theater turns into Tulsa's friendliest piano bar when Bill Nelson and Jason Sirios sit down at dueling keys and let the whole room belt showtunes together. This is a sing-along, sugar, not a recital, so nobody's checking your pitch, they just want you loud. It's the kind of night where a stranger three seats over turns into your new favorite harmony partner by the second chorus. Grab a seat near the piano so you can actually see Bill and Jason trade licks, and get there by 6:15 so you're not squeezing past knees during the opening number.
