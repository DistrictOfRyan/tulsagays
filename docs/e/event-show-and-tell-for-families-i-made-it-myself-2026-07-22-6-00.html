Show-and-Tell for Families: I Made It Myself!

Wednesday, July 22 · 6:00 PM

Zarrow Library

Show-and-Tell for Families: I Made It Myself! Turns Zarrow Regional Library into a stage for whatever anybody's been building, sewing, painting, or piecing together lately. Nobody's judging craftsmanship here, sugar, this is a room built entirely around showing up proud of something small. You can bring your own creation to share or just settle in and watch the parade of homemade wonders go by. Arrive right at 6pm so you catch every single show-and-tell from the start, that's where the best surprises always land first.
