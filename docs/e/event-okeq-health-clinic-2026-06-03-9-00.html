OKEQ Health Clinic

Wednesday, June 3 · 9:00 AM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

The OKEQ Health Clinic at the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center offers the rare thing, healthcare that feels safe and affirming from the moment you walk in. Wednesday hours run from 9 AM, with appointments made at least 24 hours ahead, and they accept Blue Cross Blue Shield along with a self-pay option for patients 18 and up. So much of the stress around care comes from not knowing if a provider will get you, and here that question is already answered. This is a steady, welcoming place to take care of yourself. Book your slot a day ahead and aim for a morning appointment when the clinic is quietest and the day still feels open.
