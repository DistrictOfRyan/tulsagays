Heartstopper Forever

Saturday, August 8

Twisted Arts Tulsa

Twisted Arts Tulsa is hosting Heartstopper Forever, a celebration for anyone who's ever cried happy tears over Nick and Charlie finding each other in a school hallway. Come as you are, this crowd isn't judging your fandom level, whether you've reread the whole series or just love watching two boys fall for each other on screen. It's the kind of gathering where being early pays off, because the best moments and the best spots happen before the space fills in. Best time to go: get there a little ahead of start time and grab a seat near the front, that's where the good stuff happens first.
