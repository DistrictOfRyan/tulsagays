Little Garden Explorers

Wednesday, August 26 · 9:30 AM

Philbrook Museum of Art

Little Garden Explorers at Philbrook Museum of Art is about as low-pressure as outings get, come whenever you're ready at 9:30am, wander the gardens as long as you like, and leave the moment you're done, no itinerary required. It's built for exploring at your own pace among the museum's grounds, so there's no group activity to opt into and no small talk you didn't sign up for. That kind of freedom is rare for a "should I go out today" morning, sugar, you get all the benefit of being out in a beautiful place with none of the social math. Go right at 9:30 when it opens, the gardens are coolest and quietest before the day heats up, and you'll have the paths mostly to yourself.
