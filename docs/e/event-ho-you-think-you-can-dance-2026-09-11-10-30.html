Ho You Think You Can Dance

Friday, September 11 · 10:30 PM

Club Majestic

Tulsa Eagle's White Party with DJ Haze turns the whole room into one glowing sea of white under the lights, and the vibe is pure "be here, not perform here." DJ Haze keeps the set moving without ever needing you to prove anything on the floor. This is the kind of night where you can lean on the bar, nurse a drink, and just watch your community glow around you. Best time tip: wear white (obviously, darling), arrive by 9:30 before the crowd thickens, and grab a spot near the bar where you can people-watch without ever feeling on display.
