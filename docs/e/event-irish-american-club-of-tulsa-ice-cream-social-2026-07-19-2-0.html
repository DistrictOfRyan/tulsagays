Irish American Club of Tulsa Ice Cream Social

Sunday, July 19 · 2:00 PM

The American Legion

Join us for sweet treats, good company and Irish tradition at the Irish American Club of Tulsa’s Annual Ice Cream Social! Enjoy a variety of ice cream, connect with friends old and new, and settle in for an afternoon of Irish storytelling as we share tales and history that have been passed down through generations. Whether you're celebrating your Irish heritage or simply looking for a fun family-friendly outing, there's something for everyone at this beloved summer tradition. We look forward to seeing you there! ☘️🍨📚 It's a Sunday thing, around 2:00 PM, over at The American Legion. Bring your whole personality. That's the only ticket this town really checks.
