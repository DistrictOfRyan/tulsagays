TFA + THE VAULT PRESENT COCKTAIL CLASS TULSA MUSIC MONTH: Iconic Tulsa Music Venues Thursday, July 23, 2026 $55.00 | Event Time: 6:30-8:30 Join Tulsa Foundation for Architecture in partnership with The Vault for a special TULSA MUSIC MONTH: Iconic Tulsa Music Venues themed cocktail class! Mark your calendar, because we feature a variety of programs and events throughout the year. Tulsa has a rich architectural heritage and TFA is passionate about sharing our knowledge with you! At this event, you will learn more about the history of iconic Tulsa music venues while enjoying themed cocktails and hors d'oeuvres in a unique, mid-century modern atmosphere. Featured venues include: The Historic Big 10 Ballroom, Cimarron Ballroom, Cain's Ballroom, and The Nine of Cups/Magician's Theater! TICKET INFORMATION: Cost for this event is $55 per person.
