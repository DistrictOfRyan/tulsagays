Friday Happy Hour at Tulsa Eagle

Friday, October 16 · 2:00 PM

Tulsa Eagle

Friday Happy Hour at Tulsa Eagle is the no-fuss kind of good time, just a patio, a drink in your hand, and familiar faces drifting in after work. Nothing to perform here, baby, you can post up at the bar solo and still end up in three different conversations by your second beer. It runs a full six hours so there's no wrong time to show up. Swing by right around 5 or 6 when the after-work crowd rolls in and the energy's at its warmest.
