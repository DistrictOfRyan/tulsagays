Saturday Morning Birders

Saturday, July 11 · 8:00 AM

Lake Yahola Monument

The Saturday Morning Birders meet on the second Saturday of every month except Jul. & Aug. Anyone who would like to bird with us (whatever your skill level!) is welcome. Meet at the monument at Lake Yahola at 8:00 a.m. in Mohawk Park, 3730 Mohawk Blvd (just north of the water plant). From there the destination will vary each month, based on the weather, and current birding conditions. For more information contact leader Terry Mitchell at 918-697-3198 It's a Saturday thing, around 8:00 AM, over at Lake Yahola Monument. Bring your whole personality. That's the only ticket this town really checks.
