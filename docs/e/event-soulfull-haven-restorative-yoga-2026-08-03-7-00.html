Soulfull Haven - Restorative Yoga

Monday, August 3 · 7:00 PM

Guthrie Green

Soulfull Haven brings a Restorative Flow session to Guthrie Green on Monday evening, the slow, supported kind of yoga where you sink into a pose and just stay a while. No flexibility required, no one's watching your alignment, this is about letting your nervous system finally exhale after a long day. Outdoors at Guthrie Green means string lights, open sky, and a breeze doing half the relaxing for you. Best time to have it: arrive a few minutes early, grab a mat spot toward the back or side of the lawn, and bring a light blanket, that grass gets cool once you're lying still.
