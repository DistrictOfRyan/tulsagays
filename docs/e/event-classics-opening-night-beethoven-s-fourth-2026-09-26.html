CLASSICS: OPENING NIGHT - BEETHOVEN'S FOURTH

Saturday, September 26

Tulsa Symphony

The lights dim, the strings tune up, and for one hour Tulsa Symphony hands you Beethoven's Fourth like a permission slip to just sit and feel something. This is Classics Opening Night, darling, the kind of date you can keep with yourself, no small talk required, no outfit crisis necessary. You can dress up in your finest or show up exactly as you are, because the music does not care and neither does anyone else in that hall. It is a full, rich hour and then you are free to go, easy as that. Get there twenty minutes early to grab a seat with a clear sightline to the strings section and let the hall itself settle you before the first note ever plays.
