Midweek Market at Santa Fe Square!

Wednesday, July 22 · 4:30 PM

Midweek Market at Santa Fe Square! - Tulsa Arts District

Midweek Market at Santa Fe Square fills the Tulsa Arts District with local artists and makers set up right there in the square, easy to browse at your own pace. The talent here is the real draw, darling, these are working artists selling actual good stuff, not just craft fair filler. It's an easy, low-pressure way to be around people without needing a plan or a group, just wander the booths and see what catches your eye. Bring cash and a little more willpower than usual, because you will find something you didn't know you needed, and get there closer to 4:30 before the good booths sell through.
