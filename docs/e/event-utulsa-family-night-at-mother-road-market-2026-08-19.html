UTulsa Family Night at Mother Road Market

Wednesday, August 19

University of Tulsa

UTulsa's Family Night takes over Mother Road Market, turning the food hall into a mix of local vendors, artists, and that easy market energy that works solo or with a full crew in tow. Every stall is a tiny temptation, and the artists slinging their work here are talented enough to empty your wallet before you notice. Graze a little of everything instead of committing to one table right away, that's how the cash you brought stretches the furthest. Best time to have the best time: arrive early evening before the dinner rush hits so you can grab a table and actually see the art before the crowd blocks the view.
