Gaymer Night

Monday, November 2 · 8:00 PM

Tulsa Eagle

Gaymer Night at the Tulsa Eagle starting at 8 is exactly what it sounds like, consoles and controllers and a room full of people more focused on the screen than on you, which, honey, is the whole appeal if crowds wear you out. Nobody's checking whether you rolled up solo, you just grab a seat near the action and watch for a while before anyone expects you to pick up a controller yourself. The people watching alone is worth the trip, there's always one friendly trash talker who'll pull you in without you having to ask. Best time tip: get there close to 8 when the good consoles are still open, and bring your own controller if you're picky about the feel of one.
