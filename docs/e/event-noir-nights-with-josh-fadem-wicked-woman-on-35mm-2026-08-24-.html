Noir Nights with Josh Fadem: Wicked Woman on 35mm

Monday, August 24 · 7:00 PM

Circle Cinema

Noir Nights with Josh Fadem returns to Circle Cinema for a one-night-only 35mm screening of Wicked Woman, the 1953 noir that started the whole series. Seeing it on real film, not a digital scan, is the difference between watching a black-and-white movie and watching an actual artifact flicker back to life on the wall in front of you. Josh Fadem's introductions are half the fun, so this isn't just a screening, it's a little show before the show. Get there in time for his intro instead of slipping in during the trailers, you'll want to hear the setup before the lights go all the way down.
