Festival Night: Nov. 27

Friday, November 27 · 5:30 PM

Philbrook Museum of Art

Festival Night turns the Philbrook's gardens and galleries into something festive without demanding you be the life of the party. You can slip in, hug the edges of a room, and still catch the whole atmosphere, the lights, the art, the general sparkle of the place. It's a come-as-you-are kind of night, baby, no performance of extroversion necessary. Arrive right at 5:30 before the crowd thickens, you'll get the gardens at golden hour and first pick of a quiet corner to post up in.
