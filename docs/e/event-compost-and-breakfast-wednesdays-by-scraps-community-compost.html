Compost and Breakfast Wednesdays! (by Scraps Community Composting)

Wednesday, June 24 · 10:00 AM

Wildflower Cafe

Picture this: a warm breakfast at Wildflower Cafe followed by actually doing something good for the planet with your own two hands, and the whole thing wraps before noon. Scraps Community Composting runs this weekly ritual every Wednesday around 10 or 10:30 AM, and it is both easier and more social than you'd expect, darling. You eat, you show up, you compost, and you leave feeling like the environmentally-responsible person you've always secretly wanted to be. Bring any kitchen scraps you've been collecting at home in a tote bag, introduce yourself to whoever's sitting nearby, and let this low-key Wednesday ritual become the thing you actually look forward to midweek.
