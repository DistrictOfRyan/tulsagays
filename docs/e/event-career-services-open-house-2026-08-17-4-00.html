Career Services Open House

Monday, August 17 · 4:00 PM

NORTH HALL

Career Services welcomes you to NH 103 for our beginning of the semester open house. During this time, meet the Career Services Coordinator (Natasha Coffelt), learn of the many services we provide, sign up for one-on-one career services assistance, get a free T-shirt and more. Get a head start on preparing your application materials for your summer internship or your future job as many employers will begin posting opportunities beginning Sept. 1. It's a Monday thing, around 4:00 PM, over at NORTH HALL. Put on the look, bring a friend or bring your nerve, and let Tulsa show you a good time.
