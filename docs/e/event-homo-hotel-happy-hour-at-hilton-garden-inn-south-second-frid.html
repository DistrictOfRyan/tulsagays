Friday, September 11 · 11:00 PM

Tulsa Hilton Garden Inn South

New venue means new corners to discover and new faces who might not usually make it to the regular spot. Best time to have the best time: double check you've got the right Friday and address before you head out, then arrive early enough to scope the room before it fills in.
