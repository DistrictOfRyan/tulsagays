Queer Crafters Club

Thursday, June 11 · 6:00 PM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

Queer Crafters Club is the gentlest possible excuse to leave the house on a Thursday. From 6 to 8 PM at the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center, LGBTQIA+ crafters of every skill level gather to knit, paint, bead, glue, and gossip. You do not need to be good at anything, and you do not even need to bring a project, there is usually a spare set of supplies and a spare seat at the table. Best-time tip: walk in around 6 when folks are still setting up, claim a chair next to whoever looks friendliest, and let the small talk happen over a craft instead of forced eye contact. It recurs most Thursdays, so if this one sneaks up on you, there is always next week.
