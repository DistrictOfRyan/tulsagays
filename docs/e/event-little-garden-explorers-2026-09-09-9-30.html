Little Garden Explorers

Wednesday, September 9 · 9:30 AM

Philbrook Museum of Art

Little Garden Explorers at Philbrook is a gentle morning in the gardens, built for slipping in quietly, finding your own patch of shade, and letting the activity come to you instead of the other way around. There's no small talk quota here, sugar, no dress code drama, just growing things and a room that's happy to let you exist at your own pace. Philbrook's grounds are gorgeous this time of year, so even a quiet morning on the edges feels like a full outing. Arrive right at 9:30 when it opens and drift toward the back garden paths first, that's where the crowd thins out fastest.
