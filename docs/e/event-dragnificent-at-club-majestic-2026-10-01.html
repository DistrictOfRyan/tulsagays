DRAGNIFICENT! at Club Majestic

Thursday, October 1

DRAGNIFICENT! Turns an ordinary Thursday into a full-blown drag show at Club Majestic, hosted by the one and only Shanel Sterling. The lineup mixes Tulsa's own local performers with visiting guests, so you're getting hometown favorites and fresh faces in the same night. It's the perfect reason to turn a weeknight into something worth getting dressed up for. Best time to grab a great seat: arrive a little early before the house fills in, and bring some cash for tipping the queens.
