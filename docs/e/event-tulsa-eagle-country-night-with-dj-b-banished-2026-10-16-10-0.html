TULSA EAGLE COUNTRY NIGHT WITH DJ B-BANISHED

Friday, October 16 · 10:00 PM

Tulsa Eagle

Tulsa Eagle Country Night with DJ B-Banished brings boots, belt buckles, and a genuinely great country-leaning set to the dance floor at 1338 E 3rd St. It's the kind of night where strangers end up two-stepping together by the second song, and a bad week gets danced right out of your system. DJ B-Banished knows how to read a floor, building the energy instead of dumping it all at once, so the room keeps filling as the night goes on. Show up about an hour in, once the floor's warmed up and the crowd's loosened, that's when the best dancers come out and the whole place feels like one big line dance waiting to happen.
