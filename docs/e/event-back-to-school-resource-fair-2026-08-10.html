Back to School Resource Fair

Monday, August 10

Youth Services of Tulsa

Back to School Resource Fair at Youth Services of Tulsa, 311 S Madison Ave. Community festivals are exactly what Tulsa needs more of: reasons for different kinds of people to be in the same outdoor space at the same time, doing the same thing, which is looking at things and eating food and talking to strangers and being reminded that your city is more interesting than your usual route through it suggests. The queer community shows up for local festivals because we understand that visible participation in the life of your city is how you build the version of it you actually want to live in. Walk slowly. Talk to vendors. Try the food from the booth you've never tried. Buy something local. Leave having learned at least one thing you didn't know.
