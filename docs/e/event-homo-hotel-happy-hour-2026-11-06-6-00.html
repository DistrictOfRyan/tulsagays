Homo Hotel Happy Hour

Friday, November 6 · 6:00 PM

Homo Hotel

The Homo Hotel's First Friday happy hour is the Tulsa tradition that turns "maybe I'll go" into "I'm already there." It's low stakes, high camp, and the kind of crowd where small talk turns into real talk before your drink's empty. Nobody's performing, nobody's rushing, it's just good people and a good room on a Friday night. Get there right at 6 while the room's still filling in, that's your easiest window to land a conversation before it gets loud and lovely.
