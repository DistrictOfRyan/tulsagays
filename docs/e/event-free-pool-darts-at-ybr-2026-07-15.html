Free Pool & Darts at YBR

Wednesday, July 15

Yellow Brick Road

The neon at Yellow Brick Road hums just right on pool and darts night, the jukebox stacked with exactly the songs you'd pick yourself, and the drinks won't wreck your budget. Post up at the bar, sugar, and let the room come to you, this crowd is easy and the games are free so there's zero pressure to be good at either one. Buy the stranger next to you their next round, darling, that's how "the bar with good music" turns into "the bar where you know people." Best time to have the best time: swing by early evening to grab a table before the boards and cues get claimed, then let the night stretch.
