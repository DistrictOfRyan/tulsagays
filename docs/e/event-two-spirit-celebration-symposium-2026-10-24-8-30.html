Two Spirit Celebration: Symposium

Saturday, October 24 · 8:30 AM

Tahlequah Public Library

The middle chapter of the Two Spirit Celebration is a Saturday-morning symposium at the Tahlequah Public Library, 8:30 AM to 12:30 PM. This is the learning heart of the weekend: Two-Spirit history, culture, and lived experience shared out loud in a warm, welcoming room, open to Native and non-Native folks alike. It is free, alcohol and drug free, and there is no charge for food or activities. Best-time tip for the shy ones: a library is the gentlest possible place to walk into something new, so come near the 8:30 start, grab a seat toward the back, and let yourself just listen, nobody is going to put you on the spot. General info at tinyurl.com/ipctahlequah.
