All Nations 2S Powwow

Friday, October 23 · 4:00 PM

Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

Sugar, All Nations 2S Powwow is honoring Two-Spirit community right there at the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center, and that drumbeat is going to land in your chest the second you walk in. This is a starter social in the best way: low stakes, a genuinely friendly room, and an easy exit if the crowd ever feels like too much. Watching the regalia and the dancing up close, in a space built for you, is the kind of thing that sticks with you long after you've driven home. Best time to go: get there right at 4, grab a spot along the wall near the dance floor, that's where you'll catch all the detail without getting swallowed by the crowd.
