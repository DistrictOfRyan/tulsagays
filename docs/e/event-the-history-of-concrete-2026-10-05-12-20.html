The History of Concrete

Monday, October 5 · 12:20 PM

Circle Cinema

This one's a short documentary screening at Circle Cinema where a filmmaker takes the earnest, over the top sincerity of Hallmark movie writing workshops and applies it, dead serious, to pitching concrete as the next great documentary subject, sugar. It's exactly as delightfully weird as it sounds, a little meta, a little absurd, and over fast enough that you can still make it home for dinner. Circle Cinema's arthouse crowd is used to oddball shorts like this, so nobody's going to blink if you show up solo with popcorn and a curious eyebrow raised. Best time to go: catch the 12:20 showtime and grab a seat a few rows back, center, so you get the full effect of the deadpan narration without craning your neck.
