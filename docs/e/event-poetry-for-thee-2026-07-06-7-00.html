Tulsa Gays · LGBTQ+ Event

Poetry for Thee

Monday, July 6 · 7:00 PM

Noise Town

Round robin format: one poem per round, two rounds with discussion about what's shared in between. A small intimate gathering. From one of the co-owners of the multi use space called Noise Town Tulsa- "We mostly do private music lessons, recording projects and host all ages shows on weekends. We also have been hosting a monthly poetry meet up since we swung the doors a few years ago. 1st Monday of each month! It’s free, and usually a pretty chill and laid back hang. Between 10- 20 people on average but at times it’s been larger. Dip in for part or all and get out of the house if you’d like!" It's a Monday thing, around 7:00 PM, over at Noise Town. Dress how it makes you feel, roll in a little early, and thank yourself later, sugar.
