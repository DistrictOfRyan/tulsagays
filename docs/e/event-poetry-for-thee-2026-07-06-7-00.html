Poetry for Thee

Monday, July 6 · 7:00 PM

Noise Town

Picture a circle of people at Noise Town, the kind of quiet that falls right before someone reads something true out loud. Poetry for Thee runs a round robin format: one poem per person, per round, two rounds total, with a real discussion in between about what everyone just shared. This is a small, intimate gathering, so bring something you've written, or bring a poem you love from someone else, or just come to listen and let the words land. The discussion between rounds is where this one truly opens up, so don't bolt after your poem, honey, that's when the real connection starts. Arrive a few minutes before 7 so you can settle in before the first round begins.
