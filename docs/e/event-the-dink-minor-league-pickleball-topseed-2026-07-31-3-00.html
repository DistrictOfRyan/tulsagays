The Dink Minor League Pickleball @ TOPSEED...

Friday, July 31 · 3:00 AM

Bounce

The Dink brings its Minor League Pickleball action to Bounce for a TOPSEED matchup, and darling, watching a well-placed dink shot land is oddly one of the most satisfying things you'll see all week. It's a spectator sport built for easing in solo: grab a seat, watch the rallies, and cheer when the crowd does. You don't need to know a single rule to get swept up in it. Best time to go: arrive early to grab courtside seats before the good sightlines disappear.
