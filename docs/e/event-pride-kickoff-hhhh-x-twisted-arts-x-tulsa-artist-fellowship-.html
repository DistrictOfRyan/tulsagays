Pride Kickoff: HHHH x Twisted Arts x Tulsa Artist Fellowship

Friday, June 5 · 5:00 PM

Tulsa Artist Fellowship

Sweetheart, this is not the night to stay home. The Tulsa Artist Fellowship First Friday becomes a full-blown Pride kickoff, with Homo Hotel Happy Hour and Twisted Arts joining the Fellowship for the very first time. Avery Marshall, a Black trans queer Tulsa artist, sings live at 7, a nine-queen drag show follows at 8, and a charity raffle runs all night for Queer Lit Collective's second birthday. Open studios, fresh art, music, and drag, all stitched into one downtown night, and it is completely free. Best-time tip for the shy ones: roll in around the 5 o'clock happy hour, start at the far end of the studios to beat the crowd, then post up near the stage before the queens come out.
