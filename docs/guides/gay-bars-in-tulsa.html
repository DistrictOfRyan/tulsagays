Frequently asked

How many gay bars are in Tulsa?

Tulsa has several dedicated LGBTQ+ bars including Tulsa Eagle, Yellow Brick Road (the state's lesbian bar), and Club Majestic, plus queer-friendly lounges like DVL.

What is the lesbian bar in Tulsa?

Yellow Brick Road (YBR), on Cherry Street at 2630 E 15th St, is Tulsa's lesbian bar and one of the few remaining lesbian bars in the country.

Where is the gay district in Tulsa?

Tulsa's LGBTQ+ nightlife clusters around the Arts District (Tulsa Eagle) and Cherry Street (YBR), with the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center anchoring the community downtown.
