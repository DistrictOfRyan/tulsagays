Nothing to do in Tulsa?
Sounds like a straight person problem.

Tulsa has a bigger, busier LGBTQ+ scene than most people expect. This page rounds up the queer events in Tulsa each week, drag shows, bar nights, Pride events, and community meetups, refreshed every Monday. The scene centers on the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center and gathering spots like the Tulsa Eagle, Club Majestic, and Yellow Brick Road, Tulsa's lesbian bar.

Saturday, November 7 · 7:30 PM
Dennis R. Neill Equality Center

Gay bars near me in Tulsa

Tulsa has three dedicated LGBTQ+ bars, and they sit in three different parts of town. Club Majestic (124 N Boston Ave, downtown) is the dance club with drag shows. The Tulsa Eagle (1338 E 3rd St, Arts District) is the leather and bear bar. Yellow Brick Road (2630 E 15th St, Cherry Street) is Tulsa's lesbian bar, and everyone is welcome. OSU Tulsa Campus
🦩🦩🦩🦩🦩Super gay
The OSU Tulsa Queer Support Group meets on campus on Tuesday evenings at 6 PM, open to OSU Tulsa students and the broader community, and it is not a club meeting with an agenda or a networking event or anything that requires preparation. It is an open gathering where you can talk about what you're going through, listen to others do the same, and exist in a room full of people who already understand without needing you to explain yourself from the ground up before they'll engage with you.
