THIS WEEK TULSA GAYS

July 20 — July 26, 2026

Nothing to do in Tulsa?
Sounds like a straight person problem.

Tulsa has a bigger, busier LGBTQ+ scene than most people expect. This page rounds up every queer event in Tulsa each week, drag shows, bar nights, Pride events, and community meetups, refreshed every Monday. The scene centers on the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center and gathering spots like the Tulsa Eagle, Club Majestic, and Yellow Brick Road, Tulsa's lesbian bar.

Event of the Week
DRAG BRUNCH
Saturday, July 25 · 12:30 PM
Drag Brunch means rhinestones catching every bit of light in the room, a host who will read the crowd for filth between numbers, and performances you'll be quoting to your coworkers by Monday, darling. It's the easiest kind of night out there is, you sit, you eat, you tip, and the show does the rest of the work for you. The energy builds fast once the first number hits, so this isn't one to sneak in late for. Best time to go: post up near the runway before the show starts, that's where the tips land and the eye contact happens, and have your dollars ready early.
