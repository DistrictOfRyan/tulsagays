THIS WEEK TULSA GAYS

July 27 — August 2, 2026

Nothing to do in Tulsa?
Sounds like a straight person problem.

Tulsa has a bigger, busier LGBTQ+ scene than most people expect. This page rounds up every queer event in Tulsa each week, drag shows, bar nights, Pride events, and community meetups, refreshed every Monday. The scene centers on the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center and gathering spots like the Tulsa Eagle, Club Majestic, and Yellow Brick Road, Tulsa's lesbian bar.

Event of the Week
EQUALITY BUSINESS ALLIANCE NETWORKING MIXER
Thursday, July 30 · 6:00 PM - 7:30 PM
Dennis R. Neill Equality Center
This is the monthly mixer where Tulsa's LGBTQ+ business owners and professionals swap cards, ideas, and the occasional client referral. Ninety minutes, one room, real connections that outlast the cocktail napkins. You don't need a pitch deck, you just need to show up and say hello to one person you don't know yet. Best time to work the room: get there right at 6 when the crowd's still small enough to actually hear each other, grab a drink, and post up near the food table, sugar, that's where the real conversations start.
