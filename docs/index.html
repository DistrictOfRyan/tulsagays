THIS WEEK TULSA GAYS

August 3 to August 9, 2026

Nothing to do in Tulsa?
Sounds like a straight person problem.

Tulsa has a bigger, busier LGBTQ+ scene than most people expect. This page rounds up every queer event in Tulsa each week, drag shows, bar nights, Pride events, and community meetups, refreshed every Monday. The scene centers on the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center and gathering spots like the Tulsa Eagle, Club Majestic, and Yellow Brick Road, Tulsa's lesbian bar.

OSU Tulsa Queer Support Group
OSU Tulsa Campus
🦩🦩🦩🦩🦩Super gay
The OSU Tulsa Queer Support Group meets on campus on Tuesday evenings at 6 PM, open to OSU Tulsa students and the broader community, and it is not a club meeting with an agenda or a networking event or anything that requires preparation. It is an open gathering where you can talk about what you're going through, listen to others do the same, and exist in a room full of people who already understand without needing you to explain yourself from the ground up before they'll engage with you. If you're queer and in Tulsa and you've been feeling a little isolated, which is one of the most common and underreported experiences in this community, this room on Tuesday nights at the Neill Center was built for exactly that.
