THIS WEEK TULSA GAYS

August 3 to August 9, 2026

Nothing to do in Tulsa?
Sounds like a straight person problem.

Tulsa has a bigger, busier LGBTQ+ scene than most people expect. This page rounds up every queer event in Tulsa each week, drag shows, bar nights, Pride events, and community meetups, refreshed every Monday. The scene centers on the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center and gathering spots like the Tulsa Eagle, Club Majestic, and Yellow Brick Road, Tulsa's lesbian bar.

Event of the Week
HHHH FIRST FRIDAY: PFLAG TULSA FUNDRAISER
Friday, August 7 · 6:00 PM - 8:00 PM
Courtyard Downtown
August's Homo Hotel Happy Hour lands at the Courtyard Downtown on Boston Ave, 6 to 8 p. m. , and it is a fundraiser for PFLAG Tulsa, the folks who have been showing up for 2SLGBTQIA+ Tulsans and their families every month for years. First Fridays at HHHH are the easiest social on the calendar: a hotel bar full of friendly faces, no dress code, no agenda, come alone and you will not stay alone long.

Circle Cinema
🦩🦩🦩🦩🦩Gay-friendly

Urban Sketchers Route 66 Centennial Exhibit
TCC McKeon Center for Creativity
🦩🦩🦩🦩🦩Gay-friendly
Urban Sketchers Tulsa is marking Route 66's centennial with an exhibit of hand-drawn scenes of the Mother Road, on view all August at TCC's McKeon Center for Creativity. This is the good kind of outing for a homebody, no crowd noise, no small talk required, just ink and watercolor capturing neon signs and diner counters exactly like your eye would if you slowed down long enough to look. It's free-feeling, wander-at-your-own-pace, the kind of art that makes you want to grab a sketchbook yourself. Best time to go is a weekday afternoon when the gallery's practically empty, so you can stand in front of your favorite piece as long as you want without anyone rushing you along.
