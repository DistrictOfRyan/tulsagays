Event of the Week
THE PRIDE ERA DRAG BINGO
Sunday, June 14 · 3:00 PM
Bricktown Comedy Club Tulsa
Oklahomans for Equality turns bingo into a full drag spectacle with The Pride Era Drag Bingo at Bricktown Comedy Club, hosted by Porsche Lynn with Shanel Sterling and Iman Scott bringing the looks and the laughs. Doors open at 2 with the show at 3, and yes, you can actually win while the queens work the room. It is 18+ with valid ID, with premium front-row and general admission seating, and showroom guests grab at least two food or drink items. Best-time tip: buy online before prices jump at the door, get there near doors at 2 to snag a good table, and keep a dauber in each hand because these rounds move fast.

Lambda Unity Group (LGBTQ+ AA Meeting)
Fellowship Congregational Church
🦩🦩🦩🦩🦩Super gay
Lambda Unity Group is Tulsa's LGBTQ+ Alcoholics Anonymous meeting, held at Fellowship Congregational Church on June 10th (check the listing for the current time). This is a space built specifically for queer and trans people in recovery, where sobriety meets the full context of your life and nobody has to code-switch or explain themselves. AA meetings can feel intimidating the first time, but Lambda Unity tends to run warm and welcoming, and plenty of the people in that room remember exactly what a first visit feels like. If it is your first time, arrive a few minutes early, introduce yourself to whoever greets you at the door, and know that showing up is already the hardest and most courageous part.
