Nothing to do in Tulsa?
Sounds like a straight person problem.

Tulsa has a bigger, busier LGBTQ+ scene than most people expect. This page rounds up the queer events in Tulsa each week, drag shows, bar nights, Pride events, and community meetups, refreshed every Monday. The scene centers on the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center and gathering spots like the Tulsa Eagle, Club Majestic, and Yellow Brick Road, Tulsa's lesbian bar.

Gay bars near me in Tulsa

Tulsa has three dedicated LGBTQ+ bars, and they sit in three different parts of town. Club Majestic (124 N Boston Ave, downtown) is the dance club with drag shows. The Tulsa Eagle (1338 E 3rd St, Arts District) is the leather and bear bar. Yellow Brick Road (2630 E 15th St, Cherry Street) is Tulsa's lesbian bar, and everyone is welcome.
