THIS WEEK TULSA GAYS

September 21 to September 27, 2026

Tulsa Gays is a free weekly guide to LGBTQ+ events in Tulsa. The list below covers drag shows, bar nights, Pride events, and community meetups, and it is updated every Monday morning.

We found 199 queer events in Tulsa this week across the 244 sources we watch. Of the 36 LGBTQ+ organizations we track here, 34 are covered. We do not claim to have all of it.

Updated September 21, 2026

Nothing to do in Tulsa?
Sounds like a straight person problem.

Tulsa has a bigger, busier LGBTQ+ scene than most people expect. This page rounds up the queer events in Tulsa each week, drag shows, bar nights, Pride events, and community meetups, refreshed every Monday. The scene centers on the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center and gathering spots like the Tulsa Eagle, Club Majestic, and Yellow Brick Road, Tulsa's lesbian bar.

BROADWAY CLUBHOUSE
Sunday, September 27 · 6:30 PM
Dennis R. Neill Equality Center
Every month, the Lynn Riggs Theater turns into Tulsa's friendliest piano bar when Bill Nelson and Jason Sirios sit down at dueling keys and let the whole room belt showtunes together. This is a sing-along, sugar, not a recital, so nobody's checking your pitch, they just want you loud. It's the kind of night where a stranger three seats over turns into your new favorite harmony partner by the second chorus. Grab a seat near the piano so you can actually see Bill and Jason trade licks, and get there by 6:15 so you're not squeezing past knees during the opening number.
