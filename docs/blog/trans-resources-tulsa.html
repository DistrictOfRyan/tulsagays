TRANS RESOURCES IN TULSA EVERYTHING YOU NEED

Support groups, doctors who won't blink, crisis lines, legal help, and family support. Verified, with real phone numbers.

July 15, 2026 ◇ Tulsa Gays
🕒 8 min read · Published July 15, 2026 · ✓ Contacts verified July 2026

Darling, if you found this page at two in the morning with your search history looking like a small confession, I want you to put your shoulders down for a moment before you read another word. You are not the first person in this city to sit alone with a browser tab and a question too big for a group chat, and you will not be the last, and every single resource on this list was built by someone who once sat exactly where you are sitting now. Tulsa is not the coastal fantasy the internet promised you, no, but it is also not the empty, hostile flyover the internet warned you about either. There is a whole, working, funded, staffed infrastructure here for transgender Oklahomans, and most of it hides in plain sight because nobody hands you the list on day one. Consider this the list.

I did not write this from memory or from wishful thinking. Every address, every phone number, and every meeting time below was checked in July 2026, because a stale hotline number helps nobody and a wrong address is worse than none at all. Where a resource asked to stay quieter, for safety and for good reason, I kept it that way. Let's start with the room that will actually have people in it this week.

Start Here: A Room Full of People Who Get It

Weekly · First-time interview required
OkEq Gender Support Group
Every Wednesday night, the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center runs a support, education, and social group for transgender and intersex Tulsans, and on the second Wednesday of the month it splits into breakout groups by identity: non-binary, trans men, trans women, and partners of trans people. Nobody is thrown into a room cold. You call ahead, do a short first-time interview so the space stays safe for everyone already in it, and then you're in.
📍 621 E 4th St, Tulsa, OK 74120 · Wed 7:00-8:30pm · (918) 743-4297 Call during business hours to schedule your first-time interview before showing up. It is a screening, not an audition.
By referral · Kids, teens, adults, and families
Taylor Burns Counseling Support Groups
A Tulsa therapist who organizes and leads separate groups for transgender children, teens, adults, and families navigating a loved one's transition. Because the groups serve minors alongside adults, the exact location and time are only shared once you've spoken with someone directly, which is a safety choice worth respecting rather than working around.
☎ (918) 675-0206 to be connected If you're a parent calling on behalf of a kid, say that up front. There is a specific group for exactly your situation.

Health Care and Hormones, Without the Lecture

This is usually the part that scares people off entirely, so let's make it boring and practical instead. Tulsa has more than one door into gender-affirming care, and none of them require you to already have all the answers.

Ages 18+ · Appointment required
OkEq Health Clinic
Housed inside the Equality Center itself, this clinic runs hormone replacement therapy alongside PrEP, PEP, and STI and HIV screening, built specifically for the 2SLGBTQIA+ community it sits inside of. Appointments need to be made at least 24 hours ahead, and the intake form is admittedly not as gender-inclusive as it should be. Pick whatever's closest and put your actual pronouns in the reason-for-visit field. They'll meet you there.
📍 621 E 4th St, Tulsa, OK 74120 · Tue-Wed 9am-4pm, Thu 9am-12pm, Fri 1pm-4pm · (918) 938-6537
All ages welcome
Diversity Family Health
A full primary care practice built around gender-affirming care rather than treating it as a side specialty, with mental health and sexual health services under the same roof. If you want one doctor who handles your transition and your annual physical and does not need a lecture on either, this is your spot.
📍 1822 E 15th St, Suite A, Tulsa, OK 74104 · Mon-Fri 9am-5pm · (405) 848-0026
Sliding scale options
Planned Parenthood, Peoria Health Center
Yes, the same Planned Parenthood you know from every other conversation about reproductive care also runs gender-affirming hormone therapy out of its Tulsa location, with the financial assistance options the name has always been known for.
📍 1007 S Peoria Ave, Tulsa, OK 74120 · (918) 858-4661

If Tonight Is the Hard Part

Before we go any further, I need to hand you the numbers that matter most, because everything else on this page can wait until morning and these cannot.

Trans Lifeline: (877) 565-8860, a peer support line staffed by trans people, Monday through Friday, noon to 8pm Central. The Trevor Project: 1-866-488-7386, or text START to 678-678, crisis counseling for LGBTQ+ youth, every hour of every day, all year. Neither one needs you to have the right words ready.

If you are under 25, the Trevor Project line is built for exactly you. If you are anyone else and simply need a person who already understands the specific weight of being trans in Oklahoma tonight, Trans Lifeline is who to call.

Legal Help and Mutual Aid

Name changes, ID updates, and the general legal maze of existing as yourself on paper are genuinely easier with a guide than alone, and Oklahoma has two organizations built for exactly that guidance.

Grassroots · Community-funded
Trans Advocacy Coalition of Oklahoma (TACO)
A Tulsa-founded, trans-led grassroots nonprofit running mutual aid and direct assistance programs for transgender, non-binary, and gender-diverse Oklahomans across the state. They're the closest thing this state has to a trans community fund, built by the people it serves.
Find them on Facebook or Instagram @transadvocacyok Their old website domain has lapsed and now points somewhere unrelated. Their social accounts are the current, verified way to reach them.
Statewide advocacy
Freedom Oklahoma
The statewide LGBTQ+ advocacy organization keeps a dedicated Trans+ Resources page covering legal and policy navigation through Advocates for Trans Equality, plus links out to TACO, Trans Lifeline, and the Trevor Project. Not a building you visit, but a genuinely useful map of everything else on this page in one place.

For Parents, Partners, and Everyone Still Catching Up

Sometimes the person who needs a resource list is not the trans person at all, it's the mother who just got told and doesn't know what to do with her hands. PFLAG Tulsa exists precisely for her, and for every parent, sibling, spouse, and friend trying to catch up to someone they love. They meet the first Saturday of every month at 7pm, alternating between support sessions and educational ones, at Youth Services of Tulsa.

📍 Youth Services of Tulsa, 311 S Madison Ave, Tulsa, OK 74120 · First Saturday, 7pm

And if the person catching up is a kid or a teenager, Youth Services of Tulsa runs its own dedicated 2SLGBTQ+ programming and Gay-Straight Alliance support separate from the PFLAG meeting it hosts, reachable at (918) 582-0061.

"You do not owe anyone a finished story about who you are before you're allowed to ask for help becoming it."

Here is the honest truth underneath all of these listings: no single resource on this page is meant to carry the whole weight by itself. Call the support group before the clinic. Call the clinic before you assume it's out of reach. Call PFLAG's number if you only have questions and no plan yet. This city built a real, if quiet, network to catch you, one Wednesday night, one appointment, one phone call at a time. You do not have to do this by memory or by guesswork anymore. You have the list now.
